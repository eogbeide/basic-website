    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Complete a simulated professional land-survey project end to end: research deeds, title and prior surveys; define an ALTA/NSPS-style scope; establish a GNSS plus total-station control network; collect boundary and topographic observations; reduce, adjust and quality-check the data; produce field-to-finish CAD deliverables, legal-description/plat and as-built outputs; publish parcel/GIS data; incorporate one LiDAR or UAV point-cloud element; document datum, closure, tolerances, field safety and chain of evidence; then present a client/engineer quality package. Any regulated boundary opinion or certification remains under a licensed PLS where required by law.

    Proof Rubric

    Show evidence-first boundary reasoning; defensible records research; control-network design; repeatable GNSS/total-station observations; correct datum/coordinate handling; closure and error checks; consistent CAD/GIS/as-built deliverables; point-cloud accuracy and provenance; field safety and crew direction; documented QA/QC; clear client/engineer communication; and correct recognition of licensure and professional-responsibility boundaries.
