    Goal: build the scientific language and clinical habits needed to understand disease rather than memorize disconnected facts. — Year 1 performance target

    • Explain mechanisms aloud without notes; draw major pathways and anatomy from memory.
    • Use foundational science to explain a patient vignette, not just recite isolated facts.
    • Complete board-style questions in parallel with each school block beginning early in the year.
    • Build a cumulative spaced-review system so foundational material remains retrievable when organ systems begin.
