    I. SURGERY QUESTION-REVIEW ALGORITHM

    • 1. Stabilize: Is there shock, airway/breathing failure, uncontrolled bleeding, peritonitis, ischemia or sepsis that changes the sequence?
    • 2. Name the syndrome: obstruction, perforation, infection/inflammation, ischemia, hemorrhage, mass/endocrine, breast/vascular or postoperative complication.
    • 3. Decide the intervention class: observe/support, image, antibiotics, anticoagulate, drain/decompress, endoscope/IR, operate or transfer/escalate.
    • 4. Sequence: answer the next step now - not the eventual definitive procedure if resuscitation or diagnostic discrimination comes first.
    • 5. Debrief: why is the best distractor wrong? Wrong diagnosis, wrong timing, wrong stability assumption, or correct treatment at the wrong stage?

    J. 7-DAY SURGERY EXAM FINISH — K. CLERKSHIP MASTERY GATE

    • Can explain the indication, anatomy, expected course and major complications for a patient's operation.
    • Can recognize postoperative deterioration and choose the first safe diagnostic/management step.
    • Can move from trauma or acute-abdomen physiology to the correct imaging / resuscitation / source-control sequence.
    • Can complete timed mixed Surgery questions with stable accuracy and a shrinking recurrence-error pattern.
