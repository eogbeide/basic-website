    B. NON-BACTERIAL MICROBIOLOGY VIDEOS - CORE + ENRICHMENT — VIROLOGY / FUNGI / PARASITE CLOSED-BOOK CHECK

    • Viruses: envelope + genome -> entry/replication/release -> environmental stability/tropism -> latency or persistence -> diagnostic and drug target.
    • Fungi: yeast vs mold vs dimorphism; host risk; morphology/tissue clue; microscopy/culture/antigen; major cell-membrane or cell-wall treatment target.
    • Parasites: protozoa vs helminths; vector/food/water/soil exposure; cyst/trophozoite/egg/larva stage; eosinophilia; stool, blood or tissue diagnosis.
    • Choose culture, PCR/NAAT, antigen, serology or microscopy from the specimen, timing and pathogen biology - not from a memorized test list. VIDEO-FIRST | SCHOOL-EXAM + BOARD-EXAM MASTERY
