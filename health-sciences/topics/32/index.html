    CURRENT STEP 1 PHARMACOLOGY EMPHASIS

    Step 1 still tests pharmacology, but current USMLE guidance emphasizes MECHANISMS OF ACTION over detailed treatment selection, interactions, adverse effects or contraindications. School exams may test the broader drug profile, so learn mechanism deeply and attach a small set of signature clinical facts.

    DRUG-CLASS INTEGRATION CHECK

    • For every class, reduce the list to receptor/enzyme/channel -> physiologic effect -> disease effect -> signature toxicity/monitoring.
    • Antimicrobials: link microbial target to selective toxicity and the resistance mechanism that defeats the drug.
    • Cardio/renal drugs: predict preload, afterload, heart rate, contractility, conduction, volume or electrolytes before recalling names.
