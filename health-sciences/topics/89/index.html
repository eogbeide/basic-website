    B. ANTIMICROBIAL + SPECIAL-INFECTION VIDEOS — HIV / IMMUNOCOMPROMISED HOST CHECK

    - Know the HIV replication targets used by NRTIs/NNRTIs, integrase inhibitors and protease inhibitors; recognize class-defining toxicities/interactions. - Use CD4 count + symptoms + imaging/lab pattern to prioritize opportunistic infections; know which prophylaxis and treatment principles are high yield. - In neutropenia, transplant or steroid exposure, widen the pathogen differential deliberately and recognize when fungal/viral disease becomes more likely. - For every antimicrobial miss, add mechanism, spectrum, signature toxicity, resistance mechanism and the infection where it is preferred to one table.
