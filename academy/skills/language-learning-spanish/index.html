HANDS-ON MASTERY

Record a 5-minute explanation, hold a 20-minute conversation, summarize Spanish media and write a 400-word note. Compare against a CEFR can-do rubric and ACTFL-informed functional lens, log recurring errors, create retrieval cards from those errors, and repeat after two weeks.

PROOF RUBRIC

  • Comprehension at target difficulty
  • spontaneous interaction
  • intelligible pronunciation
  • vocabulary range
  • grammatical control
  • discourse length
  • reading inference
  • writing organization
  • ability to repair misunderstandings
  • documented improvement from feedback
