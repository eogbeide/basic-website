    SignalReady directionCaution direction
    Official-style trendRecent forms are stable or improving; no major content domain repeatedly collapses.A single reassuring form with unstable surrounding performance.
    Mixed-block stabilityAccuracy and reasoning remain similar when topics are mixed and timed.Large drop when switching from tutor/targeted to mixed timed work.

    - Review the assessment while the reasoning is fresh. Classify every miss and every lucky guess. - Create a domain heat map: system/discipline + error type + whether the issue is knowledge, reasoning, timing or execution. - Choose the smallest repair set that closes the highest-yield clusters, then confirm with NEW questions rather than rereading notes. - Do not translate a third-party Qbank percentage directly into a licensing-exam pass probability; use official assessment outputs and school policy. — READINESS GATE

    Proceed when school requirements are met, recent official-style assessments consistently support readiness, mixed-block timing/endurance are stable, and there is no major unremediated domain. If the signals conflict, escalate to your school's academic/advising team rather than relying on a single score or internet cutoff.
