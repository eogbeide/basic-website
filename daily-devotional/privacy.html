Zuyini App Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Zuyini mobile app (word-of-the-day, language, business frameworks, relationships, fitness, interview prep and devotional lessons) on iOS and Android. It's separate from the policy for zuyini.com, since the app works differently: there's no account, no login, and no database on our server at all.

The Short Version

No accounts, no server-side storage, no ads.

You don't create an account or sign in. Your streaks, completed lessons, journal reflections and saved items live only on your device, in local app storage — we have no database and no way to see them. The only things that leave your device are the pieces needed to generate that day's AI content, read it aloud, or answer a question you type into the AI Companion. We run no ad network and no analytics/tracking SDKs.

What Stays On Your Device

Local only — never sent to us.

Progress and streaks

Which lessons you've completed, your daily streak count, and your library of past content are stored on-device using your OS's local app storage.

Journal reflections

Anything you write in a reflection or journal prompt is saved only on your device. It is never transmitted to our server or anywhere else.

Notification preferences

Daily reminder notifications are scheduled directly on your device through iOS/Android's own notification system. No notification server or device token is involved on our end.

What's Sent To Generate Content

Only what's needed, to OpenAI, to do the thing you asked for.

The app's lessons, scenarios and practice content are generated by OpenAI's API, called from our own server (we hold the API key; your device never talks to OpenAI directly).

Choosing a topic or tapping "New"

Sends a category selection (e.g. "fitness: workout" or "language: Spanish") to our server, which asks OpenAI to generate that day's content. No personal information is included.

Tapping "Listen"

The on-screen text is sent to our server, which asks OpenAI to synthesize it into speech and returns the audio. Identical text/voice requests are cached on our server and reused across all users to keep this fast and cheap — nothing in that cache is tied to you personally.

The AI Companion (business case studies)

If you type a question to the AI Companion, that question text is sent to our server and forwarded to OpenAI to generate an answer. This is the one place in the app where you type free-form text that leaves your device.

We don't operate our own database, so none of this is logged or stored against you on our side beyond what's needed to serve the response in the moment. OpenAI processes API requests under its own API data usage terms, which (unlike ChatGPT's consumer product) do not use API content to train their models by default.

Who We Share It With

OpenAI (to generate content) and our hosting provider (to run the server).

We use OpenAI's API as described above, and Render to host the server that calls it. Render runs our code but we keep no database there either, so no user data is persisted on it. We don't sell, rent or share any data with advertisers, analytics companies, or anyone else.

Deleting Your Data

Uninstall the app.

Because everything personal to you (streaks, reflections, saved content) lives only in the app's local storage on your device, deleting the app deletes it. There's no account or server-side record for us to separately delete. If you have questions about a specific request, email manny.ogbeide@gmail.com.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

The app is intended for general audiences and is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

We'll update the date at the top.

If this policy changes in a way that affects what's sent off-device or how it's used, we'll update this page and the "Last Updated" date above. This is a plain-language policy written for a small app and is not a substitute for legal advice.
