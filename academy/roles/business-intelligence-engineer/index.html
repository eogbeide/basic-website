    Build and operate a production-grade BI product for a real or simulated business domain: define source-of-truth KPIs and metric contracts; model an analytical warehouse/lakehouse; build SQL/Python ETL with validation, observability and lineage; publish a governed semantic layer and self-service dashboard; implement role-based access and documentation; analyze one experiment or causal question plus anomaly detection; integrate an AI/agentic BI experience over governed data; then present an executive decision narrative and a runbook for quality, refresh failures and metric changes.
