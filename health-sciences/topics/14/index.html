    SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CHECK

    • Build a one-page Gram-positive / Gram-negative / atypical decision map; know morphology, catalase/coagulase/oxidase logic and oxygen requirements.
    • For each major organism: transmission/exposure -> virulence factor -> syndrome -> diagnostic clue -> first-line treatment concept -> resistance/toxin.
    • Know DNA/RNA transfer mechanisms, exotoxin vs endotoxin, culture/PCR/serology principles and infection-control basics.
    • Interleave micro with immunology and antimicrobial pharmacology; the exam usually tests the interaction rather than an isolated organism fact.
