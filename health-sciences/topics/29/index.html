    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Bacterial identificationUse stain + shape + oxygen/biochemical tests + exposure to narrow the organism; recognize atypical, acid-fast and spirochetal logic.
    Virulence / toxinsCapsules, adhesins/biofilms, exotoxin mechanisms, endotoxin, antigenic variation and intracellular survival -> tissue injury and syndrome.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — E. MICROBIOLOGY ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Classification gap - knew isolated facts but could not narrow the organism from stain, morphology, exposure or host.
    • Virulence/host gap - recognized the bug but missed why a toxin, biofilm, capsule, immune status or device caused this syndrome.
    • Diagnostic-test gap - chose a test/specimen that does not detect the suspected organism at that disease stage.
    • Genome/life-cycle gap - memorized the name but not viral replication or parasite/fungal biology that explains transmission and disease.
    • Antimicrobial/resistance gap - recalled a drug but not its microbial target, spectrum logic or resistance mechanism.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — SCHOOL-EXAM GATE

    Proceed when you can infer an organism group from stain/exposure/host/syndrome clues, choose the next diagnostic from test biology, explain toxin or resistance mechanisms, and solve mixed cases without depending on one memorized buzzword or visual cue.
