Common Questions

Quick answers.

How do I reset my streak or progress?

All progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling and reinstalling the app clears it completely, since there's no account to reset.

I'm not getting daily reminder notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's system Settings, and that the app has permission to schedule them.

How is my data handled?

See the Privacy Policy for the full picture — short version: your progress stays on your device, and only what's needed to generate a lesson or answer a question is sent to our server.
