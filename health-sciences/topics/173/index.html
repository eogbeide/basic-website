    2. Localize the lesion before recalling the syndrome name Deep explanationMotor, sensory, reflex and autonomic findings should point to root, plexus, peripheral nerve, cord or cranial-nerve level. The disease label comes after the anatomic level.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueStudents memorize classic injury names but lose accuracy when the stem changes mechanism or mixes deficits. Deficit -> territory -> structure. If you can draw the pathway, the lesion usually becomes obvious.
    3. Embryology questions are failed-process questions Deep explanationA congenital defect usually reflects abnormal timing, fusion, migration, septation, rotation, involution or neural-crest contribution. Understanding the normal sequence predicts multiple anomalies without separate lists.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueDerivative tables are easy to memorize briefly but collapse when the question asks mechanism or an adult relation. Normal step first; defect second. State what should have happened before naming the anomaly.
