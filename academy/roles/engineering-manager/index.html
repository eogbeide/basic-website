    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Operate as engineering manager for a real or simulated product area. Set team charter, expectations and 6-12 month outcomes; build a technical roadmap tied to product strategy; create hiring/onboarding, coaching and career-development mechanisms; run a design review and record architecture tradeoffs; define code-review, test and AI-assisted-development quality gates; map cross-team dependencies and delivery risks; establish engineering metrics, SLOs, on-call and operational-health reviews; lead a simulated incident and blameless postmortem; then present staffing, investment, technical-debt and roadmap choices in a senior-leadership review.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate people leadership and technical judgment together: clear priorities and expectations; coaching and talent evidence; credible roadmap/architecture decisions; code/test/AI-workflow quality controls; delivery and dependency management; measurable reliability and operational health; incident leadership and learning; resource/tradeoff decisions; healthy team signals; and concise executive communication grounded in engineering and business outcomes.
