    C. WHAT SCHOOL CLINICAL-SKILLS ASSESSMENTS COMMONLY REQUIRE — History taking

    Establish rapport, identify agenda, characterize symptoms, obtain relevant background/social context, summarize and close.

    C. WHAT SCHOOL CLINICAL-SKILLS ASSESSMENTS COMMONLY REQUIRE — Physical examination

    Consent/exposure/positioning, correct sequence and technique, patient comfort, recognition of abnormal findings and summary.

    Communication

    Empathy, plain language, teach-back, shared decisions, interpreter use, difficult conversations and professional boundaries.

    Presentation/documentation

    Accurate concise oral presentation; problem-oriented note; separate facts from interpretation; acknowledge uncertainty.

    Safety/teamwork

    Hand hygiene, identity checks, escalation, SBAR/closed-loop communication, medication/allergy awareness and infection prevention.

    D. 7-DAY OSCE / CLINICAL-SKILLS REVIEW CYCLE — Days 1-2

    Rehearse opening, agenda, HPI and complete/focused history aloud; practice transitions until they sound natural rather than scripted.

    D. 7-DAY OSCE / CLINICAL-SKILLS REVIEW CYCLE — Days 3-4

    Physical-exam stations. Use a checklist once, then repeat from memory and verbalize what each maneuver is assessing.

    Day 5

    Documentation + oral presentation. Present the same case in 7-10 min, 3-5 min and 60-second formats; compare what was safely omitted.

    Day 6

    Timed mixed OSCE circuit: history, exam, counseling/difficult conversation, handoff and note. Use faculty/peer checklist feedback.

    Day 7

    Targeted repair only. Repeat failed behaviors on a NEW case; do not restart the entire checklist if the weakness is specific.

    E. CLINICAL-SKILLS ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Coverage/technique gap - omitted a required domain or performed the maneuver with incorrect sequence, positioning or interpretation.
    • Communication gap - checklist complete but rapport, empathy, signposting, plain language or shared understanding failed.
    • Synthesis gap - gathered data correctly but could not summarize, prioritize, localize or formulate an assessment.
    • Professional/safety gap - confidentiality, consent, escalation, identity, hand hygiene or supervision boundary was missed.

    SCHOOL-EXAM / OSCE MASTERY GATE

    • Proceed when you can complete unfamiliar standardized-patient stations within time, preserve rapport while using a systematic structure, perform core exam maneuvers safely, and convert findings into a concise presentation/note.
