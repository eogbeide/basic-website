Community Rules

Be kind. No exceptions.

These rules cover everything people share in Zuyini: Community posts, Study Circles, challenge groups, book, film and game clubs, and Couples Together. By joining, you agree to them. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Not allowed

Harassment, bullying or threats; hate speech or attacks on anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality or disability; sexual or violent content; spam, scams or advertising; sharing anyone's personal details; and anything illegal.

Report and block

Report any post from its menu, and block anyone so you no longer see them. Posts are filtered for blocked words and links, and a post is hidden automatically when several members report it. We review reports and remove content, and the accounts of people who break these rules.

Tell us

To report something urgent, email manny.ogbeide@gmail.com. You can delete your community account at any time in the app.

Common Questions

Quick answers.

How do I reset my streak or progress?

Progress is stored on your device, and a backup copy is saved every few minutes with your device's anonymous account, together with your token balance. To bring everything back on a new device, or after clearing your browser, enter the account code from the Tokens screen. Save that code somewhere safe: without it, the backup can't be reached. To start fresh, uninstall the app or clear the site's data and don't enter the code; email us to delete the old backup.

I'm not getting daily reminder notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's system Settings, and that the app has permission to schedule them.

How is my data handled?

See the Privacy Policy for the full picture — short version: progress stays on your device, with a backup copy under your anonymous account; your anonymous token account, anything you share in Community or Couples Together, and anonymous usage counts are stored on our server; and text you send to an AI feature goes to OpenAI to get an answer.
