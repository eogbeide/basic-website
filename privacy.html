Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary: we ask for your name and email only when you choose to sign up, we use it to send what you signed up for and nothing else, and everything else — your progress, your saved learning path, what you've viewed — stays in your own browser and never reaches our servers.

What This Covers

Zuyini Academy, Health Sciences Academy and Zuyini Weekly.

This policy covers zuyini.com and its Academy, Health Sciences Academy, Newsletter and Real Credentials pages. It does not cover third-party sites we link to (course providers, video hosts, certification pages) — each of those has its own privacy practices.

What We Collect

Only what you give us when you sign up.

If you fill out a signup form on the site (to get free access, join Zuyini Weekly, or both — it's the same signup), we collect:

Name and email address

Used to send Zuyini Weekly (if you're subscribed) and to recognize that you've unlocked full site access. We do not ask for a password, phone number or payment information anywhere on the site — everything on Zuyini is free.

Standard hosting logs

Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and request timestamps for security and performance. We don't access this for marketing purposes.

What Stays On Your Device

Your progress never leaves your browser.

Mastery checklists, your custom "My Learning" path, your Recently Viewed history and your unlocked-access status are all stored using your browser's local storage — not a database we control. That means:

We can't see your progress

There's no dashboard, no analytics, no record on our end of which videos you've watched or which roles you've browsed.

It doesn't sync across devices

Because it's stored locally, switching browsers or devices starts your tracked progress over. Your email signup status is separate and is what grants unlocked access on any device.

You can clear it anytime

Clearing your browser's site data for zuyini.com removes all of it. We have no copy to delete on our side.

Who We Share It With

One processor, for one purpose.

We use Kit (formerly ConvertKit) to store signup names/emails and deliver Zuyini Weekly. We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we don't run advertising or analytics trackers on the site.

Your Choices

Unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, anytime.

Unsubscribe from emails

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails; it does not remove your unlocked-access status, which is separate.

Request deletion

To have your name and email removed from our records, reach out through the contact details on our main site and we'll process the request.

Changes To This Policy

We'll update the date at the top.

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the "Last Updated" date above. This is a plain-language policy written for a small, static educational site and is not a substitute for legal advice.
