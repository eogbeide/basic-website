    A. STEP 2 CK - WHAT THE BLUEPRINT ACTUALLY REWARDS — B. COMLEX LEVEL 2-CE - DISTINCTIVE OVERLAY

    • Keep the same diagnosis/management engine, then add osteopathic principles, practice and OMT where clinically appropriate; the current blueprint sets a 10% minimum for that competency domain.
    • Clinical-presentation minimums include Musculoskeletal 13%, Wellness 12%, Nervous System/Mental Health 10%, GI/Nutrition 10%, Circulatory/Hematologic 10% and Respiratory 10%.
    • Do not isolate OMM into the final week. Integrate structural diagnosis, contraindications, treatment selection and osteopathic philosophy during every clerkship.
