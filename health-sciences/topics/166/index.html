    C. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED REVIEW-STRATEGY CONCEPTS - 6 TO 10

    ConceptWhat it really meansWhy students miss itEasiest clue

Block first, then interleaveWhen a concept is genuinely new, short blocked practice can build the schema; once the schema exists, mixed/interleaved practice trains rule selection.Learners either mix too early and create noise or stay blocked so long that they never learn discrimination.Learn the rule, then practice choosing the rule.
    7. Low-confidence corrects countA guessed or fragile correct answer can represent the same unstable knowledge as a miss and deserves review when the reasoning was weak.Percent correct hides lucky retrieval and guessing.Score the reasoning, not only the key.
    8. Repeat errors outrank isolated errorsA recurring error after repair signals a durable defect in retrieval/reasoning and should move up the priority list.Students chase every new miss equally and accumulate a giant backlog.Recurrence = priority.
    9. Compression must preserve decisionsA one-page sheet is useful only if it compresses mechanisms, discriminators, sequences or equations that help solve new problems.Beautiful summaries can become another passive resource.Compress to a decision rule.
    10. Readiness is performance, not completionFinishing videos/cards/questions is process data. Readiness requires stable transfer, timing, error control and appropriate assessment evidence.Completion is visible and controllable; competence is harder to measure.Can you perform on a new item under time?
