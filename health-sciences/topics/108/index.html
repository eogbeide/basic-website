    H. WHAT THE OFFICIAL EXAMS ARE ASKING YOU TO DO — I. CLERKSHIP MASTERY GATE

    • Can create a one-sentence problem representation for a new adult patient without copying the chart.
    • Can rank a differential and defend both the leading diagnosis and the dangerous alternative.
    • Can choose the next diagnostic or management step and explain why alternatives are premature or unnecessary.
    • Can identify when instability changes the sequence from diagnosis-first to stabilization-first.
    • Can present an assessment/plan concisely, follow results, recognize deterioration and escalate to supervision.
    • Can complete timed medicine questions with stable accuracy across mixed systems and recurring-error reduction.
