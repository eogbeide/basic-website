    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Take a technically complex AI/platform product from ambiguity to measurable launch: synthesize customer evidence; write a PRFAQ/PRD with acceptance criteria; build an outcome roadmap; produce an architecture/API map and lightweight prototype; use SQL/data to baseline the problem; define product/business metrics and experiments; create AI quality gates for accuracy, latency, cost, trust and safety; define MCP/tool integration where relevant; finish with pricing/packaging, GTM/adoption, launch-readiness, risks and an executive decision memo.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate PM judgment across customer, technical and business dimensions: evidence-backed problem choice; crisp requirements; architecture/API tradeoffs; prioritized roadmap; measurable metrics/experiments; AI eval/reliability evidence; prototype or technical artifact; launch/GTM economics; adoption results or test plan; communicate decisions, risks and what you deliberately did not build.
