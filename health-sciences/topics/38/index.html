    TRANSITION TO RESIDENCY + STEP 3 / LEVEL 3 PREVIEW

    WHY INCLUDE THIS NOW?

    A strong Year 4 curriculum should not end at the Match. It should close the gap between being a supervised medical student and functioning safely as a new resident.

    Residency readiness checklist

    • Perform concise pre-rounding, prioritize active problems and anticipate overnight changes.
    • Give safe handoffs and recognize when a patient is deteriorating.
    • Write problem-oriented notes and draft orders for supervisor review.
    • Manage common cross-cover scenarios using local protocols and escalation.
    • Understand discharge planning, medication reconciliation and follow-up.
    • Communicate uncertainty, ask for help early and document escalation appropriately.

    Step 3 / COMLEX Level 3

    Step 3 assesses independent general medical care and includes computer-based case simulations (CCS). COMLEX Level 3 assesses unsupervised osteopathic practice and uses clinical decision-making cases. NBOME has announced a one-day Level 3 format beginning January 13, 2027.
