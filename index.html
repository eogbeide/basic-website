About Me

I turn complex business problems into practical AI, product, and growth systems.

Manny Ogbeide brings more than ten years of experience across product leadership, AI and intelligent automation, supply chain strategy, business analytics, process engineering, and business transformation. His work spans Microsoft, Amazon, AWS, BHP, and consulting environments where clear strategy, strong execution, and measurable outcomes matter.

He is also a co-founder of an ASU consulting and innovation organization, where he helped startups shape go-to-market strategies, validate ideas, and develop early product concepts. Manny is the author of the Zuyini Framework, a product management book and practical framework for turning business ideas into focused, measurable execution.

His education includes an MBA from Arizona State University, an MSc in Process Engineering from the University of Nottingham, and executive management training from Yale School of Management.
