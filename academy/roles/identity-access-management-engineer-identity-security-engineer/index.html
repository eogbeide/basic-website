    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Design and implement identity for a cloud-native enterprise service. Model workforce, customer and workload identities; federate with OIDC/SAML; automate lifecycle provisioning with SCIM; enforce least privilege and conditional access; add PIM for privileged roles, phishing-resistant MFA/passkeys and a non-human identity for an agent or service.     Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate correct authentication and authorization flows; lifecycle automation; least-privilege role design; privileged elevation controls; workload/agent identity without long-lived secrets; phishing-resistant authentication; policy-as-code or repeatable configuration; auditable access reviews; high-signal telemetry; incident containment; and explicit security/usability tradeoffs with recovery and break-glass design.
