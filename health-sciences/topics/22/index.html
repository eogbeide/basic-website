    CURRENT EXAM EMPHASIS

    USMLE currently classifies Physiology as overlapping roughly 30-40% of Step 1 items, and 60-70% of Step 1 items assess application of foundational science. The board layer therefore emphasizes perturbations, graphs, compensation and disease integration - not rereading normal physiology.
