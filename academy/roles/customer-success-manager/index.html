    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own a strategic enterprise customer from post-sale through renewal/expansion: run discovery after handoff; build a success plan with verified outcomes and time-to-value milestones; instrument adoption and a defensible health model; map champions and economic buyers and run an EBR; detect and recover one simulated risk/escalation; quantify realized value and renewal/expansion logic; design an AI or agentic adoption plan using telemetry, governance and human escalation; then produce an executive account plan and forecast.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate customer-outcome clarity; measurable adoption and time-to-value; health indicators tied to retention/growth rather than vanity activity; executive stakeholder coverage and credible EBR storytelling; early risk detection with owners and recovery criteria; quantified value realization; renewal/expansion judgment; cross-functional orchestration; AI/agent adoption with change, telemetry, governance and human escalation; and evidence that customer outcomes changed, not just engagement volume.
