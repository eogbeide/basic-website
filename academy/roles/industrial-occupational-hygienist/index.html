    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Run an industrial-hygiene program for a real or simulated high-risk operation: qualitative exposure assessment and similar-exposure groups; SDS/chemistry/toxicology review; air/noise/heat/direct-read sampling plan; calibration and QA/QC; statistical exposure analysis and OEL comparison; ventilation/engineering-control design; respirator/PPE decision; medical/hearing/respiratory program triggers; control verification; exposure-data dashboard; worker/engineering risk communication; and a reassessment schedule.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate anticipation and recognition of hazards; valid sampling, calibration and QA/QC; defensible OEL and uncertainty reasoning; appropriate exposure statistics; direct-read interpretation; hierarchy-of-controls judgment; control-effectiveness evidence; multi-hazard integration; traceable exposure records; appropriate medical-surveillance boundaries; clear risk communication; and evidence-based follow-up.
