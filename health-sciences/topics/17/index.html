    PATHOLOGY FOUNDATIONS

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Learn the universal mechanisms of injury and disease that repeat across every organ system.

    Mastery checklist

    • Cell injury, adaptation and death.
    • Acute/chronic inflammation and mediators.
    • Repair, regeneration, fibrosis and wound healing.
    • Hemodynamic disorders: edema, thrombosis, embolism, infarction and shock.
    • Neoplasia: hallmarks, grading/staging, carcinogenesis and metastasis.
    • Environmental/nutritional disease and selected genetic mechanisms.

    You should be able to...

    • Explain a disease process from cause to cellular injury to clinical manifestation.
    • Differentiate acute vs chronic inflammation and predict complications.
    • Reason through thrombosis/shock using mechanisms.
    • Explain neoplastic transformation and spread.
    • Recognize classic gross/microscopic patterns conceptually.
