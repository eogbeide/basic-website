    B. QUESTION -> DESIGN MAP

    QuestionTypical design familyKey caution
    Does an intervention cause benefit/harm?Randomized trial when feasible; otherwise carefully designed comparative observational study.Feasibility does not erase confounding.
    What predicts future outcome?Prospective/retrospective cohort.Separate prediction from causal inference.
    What exposures are associated with a rare outcome?Case-control.Control selection can create major bias.
    How common is a condition/exposure now?Cross-sectional/prevalence study.Temporal sequence is often limited.
    How accurate is a test?Diagnostic-accuracy study with appropriate reference standard.Spectrum/verification bias can distort performance.
    How do people experience a process?Qualitative or mixed-methods study.Sampling and analytic rigor still matter.
    What does the total evidence show?Systematic review +/- meta-analysis.Protocol/search completeness and heterogeneity matter.
    Can a local care process improve?QI/improvement study with iterative measurement.Obtain the appropriate institutional determination; publication intent alone does not define research.
