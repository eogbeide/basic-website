    D. CROSS-MAP THE PRESENTATION TO THE COMPETENCY — 4. Dimension 2 is presentation-based, so study from the patient problem outward DEEP EXPLANATION The same disease may appear through wellness, dyspnea, pain, abnormal labs, pregnancy, mental-health symptoms or a system-specific complaint. Preparation is stronger when the

    learner builds a presentation algorithm: stability -> differential -> diagnostic threshold -> treatment -> reassessment/disposition.

    D. CROSS-MAP THE PRESENTATION TO THE COMPETENCY — WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Departmental notes encourage disease-by-disease memorization and can hide the fact that the exam starts with a patient presentation and a decision. EASIEST CLUE Name the chief presentation and next decision before naming the chapter. 5. OPP/OMM should not be siloed inside the 13% musculoskeletal category DEEP EXPLANATION Musculoskeletal is the largest individual clinical-presentation minimum, but osteopathic principles may be integrated into respiratory, GI, pregnancy, headache, wellness and other

    presentations. OPP is a Dimension 1 competency that can cross Dimension 2 categories.

    WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Learners often equate OMM with "the MSK block" and stop retrieving osteopathic reasoning once the chief complaint belongs to another organ system. EASIEST CLUE If a presentation has a plausible structural/autonomic/functional osteopathic layer, ask whether OPP changes diagnosis, treatment or safety. 6. The 5% presentation domains are protected minimums, not permission to ignore them DEEP EXPLANATION Human development/reproduction, endocrine, GU/renal/breast and integumentary presentations each retain explicit representation. Neglecting several smaller domains creates broad

    coverage holes that may also affect patient-care, communication and professionalism tasks.

    WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Students protect the 10-13% categories and then let smaller domains decay because each looks individually minor. EASIEST CLUE Protect a minimum viable retrieval set for every 5% domain, then deepen according to personal errors.
