    G. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION SHELF / COMAT CASES

    #Case patternBest exam reasoning
    1 2Crushing chest pain + ischemic ECG changes + instability. Acute dyspnea + hypoxemia + unilateral leg swelling.Recognize an acute coronary emergency; stabilize and activate the urgent reperfusion/ACS pathway before lower-yield confirmatory testing. Prioritize PE probability and hemodynamic stability; the next test/treatment depends on whether the patient is stable enough for diagnostic imaging.
    3 4Creatinine rises after vomiting while taking ACE inhibitor + NSAID. Hyperkalemia with ECG changes.Recognize a hemodynamic/prerenal medication pattern; assess volume, hold contributors and follow renal/electrolyte trajectory. Treat immediate cardiac membrane risk before slower potassium-removal strategies; then correct the cause and recheck.
    5 6Hematemesis + hypotension. Cirrhosis + ascites + fever/abdominal pain.Resuscitation and bleeding severity come before definitive endoscopic source localization. Suspect infected ascites; obtain diagnostic fluid testing promptly and treat the complication rather than attributing symptoms to baseline cirrhosis.
    7 8DKA with markedly low potassium. Chemotherapy patient + fever + severe neutropenia.Correct the dangerous potassium deficit before insulin-driven intracellular shift; then continue the DKA algorithm and monitor resolution. Treat as a time-sensitive neutropenic infection emergency; obtain appropriate cultures without delaying empiric broad-spectrum therapy.
    9Older inpatient becomes acutely inattentive/fluctuating overnight.Treat as delirium and search for reversible medical/medication causes rather than diagnosing dementia or primary psychiatric disease.
