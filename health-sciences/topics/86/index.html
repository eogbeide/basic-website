    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE

    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Days 1-2CBC, anemia and hemolysis. Work from indices/retic/smear/labs to mechanism; do not start with disease names.
    Days 3-4Platelets, coagulation, thrombosis and transfusion. Solve lab-pattern cases and drug/reversal questions.
    Day 5Leukemias, MPN/MDS, lymphoma and plasma-cell disorders. Build age + marker + cytogenetic comparison tables.
    Day 6Cancer biology, antineoplastic mechanisms/toxicities and oncologic emergencies. Complete a timed mixed block.
    Day 7Re-test every miss on NEW cases; redraw one anemia/coagulation/malignancy map from memory before targeted repair.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — E. HEME/ONC ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    INDEX/RETIC - wrong anemia branch | SMEAR/MARKER - missed morphology/flow clue | COAG LAB - PT/aPTT/platelet/fibrinogen pattern | CYTOGENETIC - wrong molecular association MARROW vs PERIPHERAL - wrong mechanism | DRUG/TOXICITY - mechanism/reversal gap | EMERGENCY - failed urgency/first action | TEST - wrong confirmatory strategy

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — SCHOOL-EXAM MASTERY GATE

    Proceed when you can classify an unfamiliar anemia from CBC/retic/smear, interpret bleeding/coagulation labs, distinguish major hematologic malignancies from age + markers + cytogenetics, and recognize cancer emergencies before management distractors.
