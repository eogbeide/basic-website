Integrative Capstone

Complete an evidence-based medication-use analysis for a real or simulated clinical scenario. Build a mechanism and PK/PD map; compare candidate therapies; calculate and justify a dosing/monitoring plan; assess interactions, organ function, pharmacogenomic considerations and safety signals; appraise pivotal evidence; design patient- and system-level safeguards; and produce a pharmacist-style medication review plus a post-market risk-monitoring plan. No prescribing or patient-care authority is implied.

Evidence Standard

  • Mechanism accuracy
  • defensible PK/PD reasoning
  • evidence quality
  • safe dosing logic
  • interaction and toxicity awareness
  • monitoring plan
  • uncertainty
  • patient/system context
  • regulatory and post-market reasoning
  • explicit non-licensure boundaries. RELEASE QUALITY STANDARD: long-form academic instruction, explicit limitations and an inspectable capstone artifact - not just claimed
