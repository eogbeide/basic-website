    C. THE MEDICINE CORE - LEARN BY PRESENTATION, THEN BY DISEASE — D. "SICK OR NOT SICK" FIRST-PASS ALGORITHM

    • Is the patient unstable? Address airway, breathing, circulation, severe hypoxemia, shock, altered mental status, active bleeding or rapidly evolving neurologic deficits before fine diagnostic distinctions.
    • Then identify the dominant syndrome and time course: acute vs chronic, focal vs systemic, inpatient vs ambulatory.
    • Use comorbidities, medications, immune status and recent procedures/exposures to reshape pretest probability.
    • Choose the narrowest next test that distinguishes actionable branches of the differential; do not order tests without a decision they inform.
