    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Take one messy real-world problem from ambiguity to action. Define the decision, customer/stakeholder, constraints and success criteria; make assumptions explicit; build a MECE issue tree and initial hypotheses; collect quantitative and qualitative evidence; distinguish symptoms from root causes; create a causal-loop or systems model; quantify uncertainty and update beliefs as evidence changes; design and run two tests, experiments or prototypes; compare options and second-order effects; then deliver a decision memo, implementation plan, leading indicators and a trigger-based update/rollback plan.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove disciplined reasoning rather than a clever answer: precise problem definition; clean decomposition; traceable assumptions and evidence; root-cause versus correlation discipline; systems interactions and unintended consequences; calibrated uncertainty; valid experiments/prototypes; explicit tradeoffs and decision criteria; concise synthesis; implementation ownership; and evidence that new information can change the recommendation.
