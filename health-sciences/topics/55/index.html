    B. THE REPEATABLE YEAR 1 STUDY CADENCE — Same day

    After lecture/lab, close the notes and reconstruct the mechanism, anatomy map, pathway or clinical skill from memory. Re-open only to correct gaps.

    B. THE REPEATABLE YEAR 1 STUDY CADENCE — Next day

    Brief retrieval of yesterday's material + 10-20 targeted questions after the concept is learned; review every option, not only the keyed answer.

    3-7 days

    Spaced re-test using new questions/images and interleaved prior content; avoid rereading the same notes as the primary method.

    Weekly

    One cumulative mixed block + spaced review of older foundations. Track whether old material remains retrievable under time pressure.

    End of block

    Timed mixed questions + practical/OSCE when applicable + written remediation plan built from the error log.

    WEEKLY MASTERY CHECK

    • Can explain the week's major mechanisms without notes and draw the key relationships from memory.
    • Question misses are becoming narrower and more specific rather than repeating the same concept error.
