    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Take a technical or AI product from market ambiguity to measurable adoption: synthesize customer interviews, market/competitive evidence and usage data; define ICP and segments; build differentiated positioning, message hierarchy, proof points and claim guardrails; create a launch brief, GTM plan and cross-functional readiness cadence; develop sales/CS enablement plus customer proof; set pricing/packaging hypotheses; define funnel, adoption, ROI and trust metrics; run a post-launch review and use evidence to change messaging, product priorities or commercialization.

    Proof Rubric

    Show traceable customer and market evidence; crisp segmentation and ICP logic; differentiated positioning with credible proof; accurate translation of technical capability and limitations; competitive judgment without unsupported claims; executable launch ownership across Product, Sales, CS, Comms, Legal/Policy and Growth; field-ready enablement; monetization logic; measurable adoption/ROI; responsible messaging and trust controls; and a documented post-launch decision driven by qualitative and quantitative evidence.
