    PHYSIOLOGY - HIGH-YIELD EXAM REVIEW

    YEAR 1-2 | USMLE STEP 1 + COMLEX LEVEL 1 + QBANK TRANSFER

    WHY PHYSIOLOGY GETS EXTRA WEIGHT HERE

    USMLE currently classifies physiology as overlapping roughly 30-40% of Step 1 items. The exam is integrated, so normal physiology is usually tested through disease, graphs, tables, drugs and compensatory responses.
