    Embed with a real or simulated enterprise customer and own one frontier-AI deployment from ambiguity to stable production.     Prove end-to-end customer impact: sharp problem framing and success criteria; hands-on code and systems integration; grounded AI behavior; representative evals and failure analysis; safe permissions/data boundaries; reliability, latency and cost evidence; disciplined scope-speed-quality tradeoffs; customer adoption and workflow impact; clear executive/client communication; maintainable handoff; reusable abstractions; and specific field learning for Product/Research rather than a one-off demo.
