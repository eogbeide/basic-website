    J. OB/GYN QUESTION-REVIEW ALGORITHM

    • 1. Pregnancy status + gestational age: establish the context before interpreting bleeding, pain, hypertension, medications, imaging or fetal questions.
    • 2. Stability: does hemorrhage, severe hypertension/eclampsia, sepsis, fetal compromise, torsion or rupture change the sequence?
    • 3. Maternal + fetal priorities: in obstetrics, ask what is safest for both - while remembering maternal stabilization takes precedence.
    • 4. Syndrome + discriminator: painful vs painless bleeding, uterine tone, fetal tracing, cervical change, discharge, mass characteristics, age/risk and timing are often the decisive clues.
    • 5. Next step now: observation, lab/ultrasound/monitoring, medication, induction/delivery, operative management, screening/prevention or follow-up - not the ultimate step several moves later.

    K. 7-DAY OB/GYN EXAM FINISH — L. CLERKSHIP MASTERY GATE

    • Can frame an obstetric case by pregnancy status, gestational age, maternal stability and fetal status before choosing a test or treatment.
    • Can recognize time-critical obstetric emergencies and identify the first safe supervised step before definitive management.
    • Can approach pelvic pain, bleeding, contraception, preventive care and gynecologic malignancy as structured decision pathways rather than isolated facts.
    • Can complete timed mixed OB/GYN questions with stable accuracy and fewer repeated management-sequencing errors.
