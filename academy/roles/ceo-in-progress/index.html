    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Build a board-ready CEO operating system for a real or simulated enterprise. Write a strategy kernel with explicit choices and a stop-doing list; create a 3-year value-creation thesis and capital/resource allocation map; design the top team and operating rhythm; build a KPI/value tree and monthly/quarterly review cadence; prepare a board calendar plus decision memo; map critical stakeholders and moments of truth; run a disruption/AI scenario and portfolio-reallocation decision; create succession and leadership-development mechanisms; and finish with a 100-day CEO time/energy system and renewal agenda.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate enterprise judgment rather than executive theater: coherent strategic choices; explicit capital/resource logic; customer and business-model economics; strong top-team/culture mechanisms; decision-ready metrics and operating cadence; board-quality governance; stakeholder trust; scenario and reinvention discipline; measurable AI-transformation value; succession depth; and evidence that CEO time is concentrated on work only the CEO can do.
