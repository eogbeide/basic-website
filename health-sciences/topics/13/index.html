    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — E. ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Mechanism gap - knew the disease name but could not explain the molecular/cellular step.
    • Directionality/process gap - confused template/coding strand, 5'/3' synthesis, splicing or translation order.
    • Inheritance gap - recognized a disease but misread the pedigree or recurrence probability.
    • Genetics-vocabulary gap - penetrance, expressivity, anticipation, imprinting, heterogeneity or mosaicism.
    • Test-selection gap - chose a technology that cannot detect the suspected mutation/chromosome abnormality.
