    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Build and govern a cross-product AI portfolio and shared platform for a public-health or enterprise context. Define the strategy, outcome thesis and platform charter; inventory 6-8 initiatives; create stage-gate and scaling-readiness criteria; model budgets, resources and investment memos; run human-centered research including low-bandwidth and accessibility constraints; define shared platform capabilities and APIs plus an integration map for FHIR, DHIS2 and OpenMRS; write data-access, privacy, security and evaluation requirements; create dashboards for quality, adoption, cost and reliability; design the stakeholder and country/partner scale-up plan; pilot one use case; then make evidence-backed scale, pivot or stop decisions in an annual portfolio review.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate portfolio and platform judgment together: clear strategy and sequencing; evidence-backed stage gates and resource allocation; platform-as-product adoption; human-centered design and trust; standards-based interoperability; AI evaluation and data governance; privacy/security controls; scaling and adoption grounded in operating context; partner and government influence; measurable outcome, cost and reliability evidence; explicit scale/pivot/stop decisions; and an executive narrative that connects investments to impact.
