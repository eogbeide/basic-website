    RESOURCE PRINCIPLE

    One strong explanatory source + one primary Qbank + official assessments beats collecting five overlapping resources. Add a second source only to solve a specific weakness.

    NEW RESOURCE STANDARD - VIDEO FIRST, EXAM EVIDENCE SECOND

    • Student-facing teaching links must be direct videos or true video libraries - not university syllabi, curriculum descriptions, webinars or course-registration pages.
    • University curricula (UW, Stanford, Harvard, Hopkins, PNWU, etc.) are benchmark evidence only; they define coverage, not the learner's teaching resource.
    • Each course now separates DEEP CONCEPT videos from HIGH-YIELD EXAM REVIEW videos and from QBANK/OFFICIAL ASSESSMENT tools.
    • Step 1 is pass/fail, so there is no current Step 1 percentile to copy. For scored exams such as Step 2 CK, we use recurring patterns from recent 270+ public score reports as community evidence - never as a guarantee.
