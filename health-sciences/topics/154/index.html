    E. WHEN TO INTERLEAVE VS BLOCK

    • Block first when the schema is genuinely new: learn the anatomy map, acid-base framework or labor stages before mixing them.
    • Interleave next when the exam asks you to choose among similar alternatives: obstructive vs restrictive PFTs, nephritic vs nephrotic, shock types, anemia patterns or causes of acute abdomen.
    • Do not interleave unrelated chaos. Mix concepts that compete for the same decision, not random subjects merely to make studying feel difficult. 2026/27 study-system expanded edition - verify school and official exam updates before testing
