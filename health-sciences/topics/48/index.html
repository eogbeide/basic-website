    YEAR 1 CUMULATIVE REVIEW - END-OF-TERM

    Four-pass cumulative review

    PassWhat to do
    1. Coverage auditVerify every Year 1 domain has been learned and questioned at least once.
    2. Mechanism reconstructionRedraw biochem, physiology, immune and path mechanisms from memory; map anatomy and neuroanatomy.
    3. Mixed casesUse mixed questions so the diagnosis is not cued by the chapter title.
    4. Readiness checkUse school cumulative exam or a high-quality external basic-science assessment when appropriate; remediate by error type.

    Integrated case prompts

    • Anemia: connect hematopoiesis -> iron/B12/folate biochemistry -> RBC morphology -> physiology -> pathology.
    • Sepsis: connect microbiology -> innate immunity -> cytokines -> vascular physiology -> shock -> antimicrobial pharmacology.
    • Diabetes: connect insulin signaling -> carbohydrate/lipid metabolism -> endocrine physiology -> micro/macrovascular pathology -> drug mechanisms.
    • Neurologic lesion: localize anatomy -> explain physiology -> predict exam findings -> choose diagnostic test.

    These integrations become the scaffold for Year 2 organ-system learning and later Step 1/COMLEX review.
