    H. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED SYSTEM CONCEPTS - #6 TO #8

    # / CONCEPTDEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    6. Localization before diagnosisA neurologic syndrome becomes tractable when you first decide central vs peripheral, then level, side and pathway/territory. Disease names are downstream of anatomy.Recognizable labels such as stroke, MS or neuropathy tempt learners to skip the anatomic proof.Ask where is the lesion? and predict one companion finding before naming the disease.
    7. Crossing determines the side of the deficitMotor, dorsal-column and spinothalamic pathways cross at different levels; brainstem lesions can produce crossed cranial-nerve/long-tract patterns.Students remember pathway names but not the exact decussation level.Trace receptor -> first neuron -> crossing -> destination; then place the lesion relative to the crossing.
    8. Tempo is a neurologic diagnostic testSeconds-to-minutes suggests vascular/electrical processes; hours-days may fit inflammatory/infectious/toxic; weeks-months can suggest mass/degenerative processes, though exceptions exist.Learners focus on symptom content and ignore the time course that reshapes pretest probability.After localization, ask how fast did it evolve? before choosing the mechanism.
