    PatternWhat can failProtection strategy
    Short sleep / sleep debtAttention, working memory, retrieval, reaction time, mood regulation and error detection can deteriorate.Protect adequate sleep opportunity across the week; reduce late-night low-yield work before high-stakes days.
    Night / circadian mismatchAlertness may fall even when total sleep seems adequate; night work can create predictable vulnerability windows.Use planned sleep, light/activity timing, breaks and supervised fatigue-mitigation processes appropriate to the setting.
    Extended wakefulnessPerformance may feel "normal" subjectively while accuracy, vigilance and response speed decline.Use an external stop rule: timing, repeated errors, supervisor concern or unsafe drowsiness can override subjective confidence.
    High caffeine dependenceCaffeine may improve alertness temporarily but does not replace sleep and can impair later sleep when used poorly.Use judiciously; avoid escalating caffeine as proof that more work is safe.
    Late-block accuracy decayEndurance failure can appear only after sustained testing/clinical work.Track first-vs-last block performance; train pacing and recovery, not just content.
