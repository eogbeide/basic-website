    I. PEDIATRICS QUESTION-REVIEW ALGORITHM

    • 1. Age first: what is normal, dangerous and common at this exact developmental stage?
    • 2. Stability: does respiratory failure, shock, altered mental status, severe dehydration, ongoing seizure or toxic appearance change the sequence?
    • 3. Syndrome + discriminator: choose the few clues that separate bronchiolitis from asthma, simple febrile seizure from CNS infection, gastroenteritis from surgical abdomen, normal variant growth from systemic disease, etc.
    • 4. Age-specific next step: test, observe, treat, immunize/screen, admit or discharge with follow-up - using pediatric thresholds rather than adult reflexes.
    • 5. Safety check: weight-based calculation, contraindication, vaccine/prevention issue, safeguarding concern, caregiver reliability and return precautions.

    • Can state age-appropriate normals, growth/development trajectory and preventive priorities before interpreting disease.

    • Can complete timed mixed Pediatrics questions with stable accuracy and fewer repeated age-specific reasoning errors.
