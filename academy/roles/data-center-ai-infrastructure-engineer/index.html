Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

Design and validate a production-ready AI data-center pod/campus slice: define GPU/rack and workload assumptions; power one-line, redundancy, rack density and liquid-cooling/CDU architecture; specify Ethernet/InfiniBand/NVLink fabrics, provisioning/scheduling and storage paths; build capacity, telemetry/SLO and failure-domain models; create FAT/SAT + integrated commissioning tests and a digital-twin scenario model; quantify PUE/WUE, usable GPU capacity, bottlenecks, cost and recovery tradeoffs.

Proof Rubric

Show engineering evidence, not a diagram: defend power, cooling, rack and fabric assumptions; demonstrate capacity and failure-domain math; validate collective/network performance; automate cluster bring-up and GPU lifecycle; prove telemetry, SLOs, health checks and incident diagnosis; execute commissioning/failure tests; quantify PUE/WUE, GPU utilization, availability and cost; explain residual risks, maintainability and scale-out tradeoffs.
