    L. FINAL 7-DAY FINISH

    TimeExecution
    Days 7-5One official or high-quality cumulative assessment if appropriate -> identify the final 3-5 weaknesses -> repair with targeted mixed questions. Do not start a new encyclopedic resource.

    Day 1Light retrieval only. Confirm logistics. Stop heavy new learning early. Protect sleep and normal routine.

    L. FINAL 7-DAY FINISH — M. EXAM-DAY EXECUTION

    • Step 2 CK: think in 30-minute units. Do not let one difficult item contaminate the next block. Use the longer total break allotment strategically across the 16-block day.
    • Level 2-CE: manage 40-question sections inside two separately timed 4-hour sessions. Remember: advancing ends access to the prior section.
    • For both: answer every item, flag sparingly, protect time for the final questions, eat/hydrate predictably and treat a practice-tested break strategy as part of preparation.
    • When two answers both seem reasonable: ask which is safest and most appropriate now for this patient, at this severity, in this setting.

    L. FINAL 7-DAY FINISH — N. PROFESSIONAL-EXAM MASTERY GATE

    • Can solve mixed cases without needing the specialty label first.
    • Can distinguish stabilization, next diagnostic test, confirmatory test, initial treatment, definitive treatment and disposition.
    • Can explain why the strongest distractor is wrong in timing, indication, contraindication or setting.
    • Can sustain timed performance with decreasing repeat errors across official-style assessments.
    • DO track: OPP/OMM and osteopathic framing remain retrievable without sacrificing the broad clinical-management core. NBME CCSSA | NBME Self-Assessments | COMSAE format | NBOME Bulletin | USMLE Step 2 CK
