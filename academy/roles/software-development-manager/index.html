    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Manage a production software area from roadmap through live-site. Define the team charter, customer/product requirements and quarterly commitments; create a 6-12 month technical roadmap and architecture/ADR package; establish code-review, test, security and CI/CD quality gates; map dependencies and release/rollback plans; instrument delivery metrics plus SLOs; run a failure drill and blameless postmortem; implement one guarded AI/coding-agent workflow for triage, test generation or CI failure analysis; then produce a hiring/coaching plan and senior-leadership review of delivery, reliability, technical debt and investment tradeoffs.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove end-to-end SDM ownership: engineers grow and expectations are clear; architecture scales and tradeoffs are documented; code/test/release controls protect quality; commitments and dependencies are managed with evidence; delivery metrics and SLOs drive decisions; incidents produce learning; AI agents operate inside explicit permissions and human-review gates; and customer impact, reliability, cost and team health are communicated crisply to product and senior leadership.
