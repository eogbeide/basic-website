    • 3. Syndrome + timeline: define mood, psychotic, anxiety/trauma/OCD, cognitive, substance, eating, personality or developmental pattern and apply duration/function discriminators.
    • 4. Longitudinal relationship: prior episodes, psychosis outside mood episodes, treatment response, collateral and baseline function often determine the diagnosis.
    • 5. Next step now: psychotherapy, medication, medical workup, substance-withdrawal treatment, hospitalization, safety planning, ECT/other intervention, or follow-up - choose the step appropriate to severity and setting.

    I. 7-DAY PSYCHIATRY EXAM FINISH — J. CLERKSHIP MASTERY GATE

    • Can conduct and present a focused psychiatric interview and MSE using observed findings rather than vague labels.
    • Can distinguish look-alike diagnoses using timeline, functional impairment, collateral and medical/substance exclusions.
    • Can connect psychotropic choice to indication, signature toxicity, contraindication/monitoring and emergency adverse effects.
    • Can recognize when safety, capacity, delirium, intoxication/withdrawal or medical instability changes the disposition before the final diagnosis is settled.
    • Can complete timed mixed Psychiatry questions with stable accuracy and fewer repeated timeline/management-sequencing errors.
