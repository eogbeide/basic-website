Inheritance questions are probability problems under a biological model Deep explanationFirst infer the transmission model from sex distribution, affected generations and parent-to-child pattern. Then calculate risk, and only afterward incorporate penetrance, mosaicism, anticipation or imprinting.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueRecognizing a disease association can override the actual pedigree evidence. Pattern -> genotype -> probability. Disease name is optional if the pedigree model is correct.
    8. Genetic test selection depends on lesion scale and mechanism Deep explanationLarge chromosome abnormalities, targeted loci, copy-number changes, sequence variants and repeat expansions are different problem classes. A test is useful only if its resolution and method can detect the suspected lesion.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueStudents memorize test names independently and pick the most sophisticated-sounding option. Ask what physical change you are trying to see. Size/type of lesion chooses the method.
