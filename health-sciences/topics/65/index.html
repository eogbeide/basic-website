    SHELF / COMAT REVIEW - PEDIATRICS + OB/GYN

    ShareXinf

    Pediatrics review spine

    • Growth/development and milestones; newborn care; vaccines/prevention; respiratory/GI/infectious disease; congenital disease; pediatric emergencies.
    • Always adjust differential, vitals, labs, fluids and medication reasoning for age/weight.
    • Revisit developmental milestones and immunization/prevention repeatedly because they are easy to forget between systems.

    OB/GYN review spine

    • Prenatal care, fetal testing, labor management, postpartum; hypertensive disease; bleeding; ectopic/miscarriage; contraception; infertility; abnormal uterine bleeding; gynecologic oncology.
    • Build explicit management algorithms for trimester-specific bleeding, labor progression, fetal heart tracing, preeclampsia and postpartum emergencies.
