    8. Low-confidence corrects belong in the error system Deep explanationA correct answer reached by guessing, elimination without understanding or unstable reasoning is not equivalent to a durable correct. It may fail as soon as the representation changes.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueScore reports reward the final choice, so learners ignore fragile corrects. Track correct + unsure separately from correct + well-supported.
    9. Error stability matters more than one mixed-block percentage Deep explanationA learner is improving when recurring error classes narrow and previously repaired rules remain correct on new items over time. One strong or weak block can be noisy.
    Why learners miss it Easiest cluePercent correct is simple and emotionally salient, so process trends are ignored. Ask: Are the same errors coming back?
    10. Observed skills cannot be certified by written review Deep explanationOSCE, physical-exam and OMM performance require supervised practice, feedback and school-defined competence. Written cases can support reasoning but cannot establish hands-on performance.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueLearners overgeneralize success on questions or videos to motor and communication competence. For a skill: perform -> receive feedback -> repeat.

    K. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED CUMULATIVE-REVIEW CONCEPTS - #7 TO #10 — L. MIXED-BLOCK REVIEW RULE

    For each incorrect, guessed or low-confidence correct: identify the task, name the decisive clue, reconstruct the mechanism, explain why the strongest distractor fails, tag the error type, and schedule a new-item retest.
