    E. CHRONIC NEUROLOGY - PATTERN BEFORE TEST

    DomainPattern recognitionKey diagnostic / management logic
    Parkinsonism / tremorBradykinesia + rigidity/rest tremor/postural-gait change; distinguish essential tremor, drug-induced parkinsonism, atypical features.The exam pattern matters more than a single tremor description; review medication adverse effects and fall/cognition/autonomic burden.
    DemyelinationCNS lesions separated in time/space; optic neuritis, INO, sensory/motor/cerebellar syndromes.Differentiate CNS demyelination from peripheral demyelination; MRI/CSF and syndrome context guide diagnosis.
    Peripheral neuropathy / radiculopathyLength-dependent stocking-glove vs named nerve vs dermatomal pain/weakness/reflex change.Use anatomy and reflexes to decide whether electrodiagnostics, spine imaging or metabolic/toxic workup is useful.
    Myasthenia / LEMSMG: fatigable ocular/bulbar/proximal weakness, sensation intact. LEMS: proximal weakness + autonomic clues, often improves with activity.Respiratory/bulbar weakness changes urgency; medication triggers and associated disease matter.
    GBS / myopathyGBS: progressive weakness + areflexia, often sensory/autonomic symptoms. Myopathy: proximal weakness, sensation preserved, CK/myopathic clues.Serial respiratory/autonomic assessment can be more urgent than confirming the subtype.
    Cognitive / degenerativeTempo + cognitive domain + motor/behavioral/autonomic clues; separate delirium from dementia.Acute inattention/fluctuation is delirium until proven otherwise; chronic syndromes require function + reversible-cause context.

    F. NEUROLOGIC EMERGENCY TRIGGERS

    TriggerImmediate concernDo not miss
    Declining consciousness + anisocoria / posturingRaised ICP / herniation / mass effect.Urgent stabilization + imaging/neurosurgical pathway; do not perform a nonessential procedure that delays treatment.
    Fever + altered mental status / meningismus / seizureMeningitis or encephalitis.Time-sensitive empiric therapy and appropriate imaging/LP sequence depend on stability and herniation risk.
    Back pain + weakness + sensory level or bladder/bowel symptomsCord compression, cauda equina, epidural process.Urgent spinal imaging and definitive specialty management.
    Rapid ascending weakness / bulbar symptomsGBS or NMJ crisis with respiratory failure risk.Serial respiratory mechanics, airway planning and autonomic monitoring.
    Acute painful vision loss / monocular loss / diplopia with focalOptic neuritis, retinal/vascular emergency, cranial neuropathy or brainstem disease.Localize eye vs optic nerve vs pathway; vision-threatening causes need urgent specialty evaluation.
