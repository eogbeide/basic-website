    B. COMMUNICATION + PROFESSIONAL-PRACTICE VIDEOS - CORE + ENRICHMENT — PROFESSIONAL-PRACTICE CHECK

    • Oral presentation: one-liner -> prioritized HPI -> relevant background/exam/data -> assessment -> plan/questions; omit information that does not change the receiver's model.
    • Documentation: distinguish patient-reported history from observed findings and your assessment; do not copy-forward uncertainty as fact.
    • Use closed-loop communication for urgent tasks and structured handoff/escalation; explicitly state instability and what you need from the receiver.
