    Proof Rubric

    Prove authoritative-source and ownership boundaries; measurable completeness, accuracy and provenance; safe agent permissions and human-approval gates; correct PIM write-back and downstream consistency; graph/semantic-model quality; grounded retrieval that satisfies constraint-rich customer intent rather than keyword matching; structured-data/feed discoverability; search, recommendation and conversational-AI evaluation; retail-media incrementality and closed-loop measurement without degrading customer trust; observability, rollback and cost controls; and a reusable architecture and roadmap that can expand beyond product data.
