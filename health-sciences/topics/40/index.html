    READINESS GATES - DO NOT USE A SINGLE SCORE

    GateEvidence to look for
    Content coverageAll blueprint domains have been learned at least once and tested with questions.
    Question-bank learningPerformance trend improving; explanations reviewed; old errors retested.
    External assessmentsMultiple recent official or high-quality external assessments are concordant rather than one isolated result.
    Timed enduranceCan complete blocks at target pace without a major late-block collapse.
    Error stabilityHigh-frequency weak areas are shrinking; repeated identical mistakes are uncommon.

    NO PASS GUARANTEE

    A curriculum can maximize coverage and preparation, but no resource stack can guarantee a school or licensing-exam result. Readiness decisions should use official assessments, school guidance and the learner's full performance pattern.
