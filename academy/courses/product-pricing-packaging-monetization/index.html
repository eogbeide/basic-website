    Product Pricing, Packaging & Monetization

    Design pricing and packaging that capture customer value. Learn segmentation, willingness-to-pay research, value metrics, tiers and entitlements, elasticity, conjoint and price-sensitivity methods, discounting, experimentation, unit economics and commercial rollout.

    BASIC
    • ▶Pricing for B2B SaaS — Willingness to Pay (~15 min)
      Start with the central pricing principle: connect customer value and willingness to pay to the commercial model.
    • ▶How To Price SaaS Products Accurately — Willingness to Pay (~12 min)
      See a structured pricing process covering research, segmentation, packaging and price-point decisions.
    • ▶Value Proposition Canvas Explained — Strategyzer (~9 min)
      Ground packaging in customer jobs, pains and gains before deciding what belongs in each offer or tier.

      Strengthen the economic lens: cash flows, discounting and value creation behind pricing and product investment decisions.

      Move from cost-plus thinking to value-based pricing, segmentation and quantified customer economics.
    • ▶What Is Usage-Based Pricing for SaaS? — Willingness to Pay
      Understand usage/value metrics, consumption risk, predictability, expansion behavior and when usage-based models fit.
    • ▶SaaS Pricing Explained ($1M Strategy) — Olly Rosewell
      Connect tier design, packaging, positioning and revenue strategy in a practical SaaS pricing teardown.
