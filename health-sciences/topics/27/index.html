    HEMATOLOGY / ONCOLOGY — Core checklist

    • Hematopoiesis, anemia classification and hemolysis.
    • Platelets/coagulation and thrombosis.
    • Leukemia, lymphoma, plasma-cell disorders.
    • Cancer biology, staging principles, chemotherapy/toxicities and oncologic emergencies.

    HEMATOLOGY / ONCOLOGY — Mastery outcomes

    • Interpret CBC/smear/coagulation patterns.
    • Classify anemia quickly.
    • Recognize oncologic emergencies and treatment toxicities. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    INFECTIOUS DISEASE — Core checklist

    • Syndrome-based bacterial, viral, fungal and parasitic disease.
    • Antimicrobial selection, resistance and stewardship.
    • HIV and opportunistic infection principles.
    • Vaccination, infection prevention and public-health response.

    INFECTIOUS DISEASE — Mastery outcomes

    • Choose likely pathogen and first diagnostic step.
    • Use antimicrobial mechanism + patient factors to guide therapy.
    • Recognize isolation/prevention principles. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    SYSTEM BLOCK ASSESSMENT

    Complete a targeted question set after each major topic, then a timed mixed block at the end of the system. Remediate by mechanism, not by memorizing the question.
