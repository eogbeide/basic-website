    CURRENT EXAM EMPHASIS

    USMLE Step 1 currently assigns 7-11% of items to the Cardiovascular System, while 60-70% of Step 1 assesses application of foundational science. The current COMLEX-USA Level 1 clinical-presentation blueprint assigns a 10% minimum to Circulatory and Hematologic Systems. Board review should therefore emphasize mechanism, hemodynamics, ECG/murmur interpretation and integrated pharmacology.
