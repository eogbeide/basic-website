    ClerkshipCore competency
    Internal MedicineHistory/exam, differential, diagnostics and management of common adult inpatient/outpatient disease; chronic disease; acute decompensation.

    Family MedicinePreventive care, undifferentiated outpatient problems, chronic disease, pediatrics/women's health overlap and continuity.

    Obstetrics & GynecologyPrenatal care, labor/delivery, pregnancy complications, gynecologic disease, contraception, cancer screening.
    PsychiatryDiagnostic interviewing, risk assessment, mood/psychotic/anxiety/substance disorders, pharmacotherapy and disposition.
    NeurologyLocalization, stroke, seizures, headache, movement, neuropathy, neuromuscular disease and neurologic emergencies.
    Emergency MedicineStabilization, resuscitation, undifferentiated complaints and disposition; core at some schools and Year 4 at others.
