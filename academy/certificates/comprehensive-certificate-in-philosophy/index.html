    Integrative Capstone

    Write and defend a substantial philosophical thesis on a consequential question in ethics, knowledge, mind, science, technology or political philosophy. Reconstruct at least two competing positions; formalize the core argument where useful; identify assumptions and counterexamples; develop the strongest objection; reply without straw-manning; distinguish empirical evidence from conceptual claims; and deliver both a research paper and a 10-minute oral defense.

    Evidence Standard

    Argument validity; conceptual precision; charitable reconstruction; source use; counterargument quality; distinction between empirical and normative claims; intellectual independence; clear prose; calibrated conclusions; ability to revise under criticism. RELEASE QUALITY STANDARD: long-form academic instruction, explicit limitations and an inspectable capstone artifact - not just claimed.
