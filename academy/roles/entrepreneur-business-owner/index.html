    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Launch or materially improve a real or simulated operating business. Select a customer problem and business model; interview customers and test willingness-to-pay; build a simple offer and sales process; model revenue, gross margin, cash conversion and 12-month cash flow; choose legal/funding assumptions; acquire an initial cohort of customers; document the end-to-end operating process and supplier/vendor dependencies; delegate at least one recurring workflow with controls; establish a KPI dashboard and monthly business review; implement one AI-enabled workflow with human verification; then produce a growth plan covering pricing, channel mix, hiring, capital needs, resilience and owner time leverage.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove a business can operate, not just launch: real customer evidence; coherent unit economics and cash visibility; repeatable acquisition / sales motion; pricing discipline; reliable fulfillment and service process; legal / financial hygiene; delegation and standard work; KPI-driven decisions; resilient supplier and capacity planning; responsible AI use with source checks and approvals; and a growth plan that improves both enterprise value and the owner's leverage rather than simply increasing workload.
