    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Act as strategy and operations lead for a real or simulated business unit. Write a concise strategy kernel with explicit choices and a stop-doing list; build 12-month priorities, budget/headcount/resource allocation and a business case; define a KPI tree and weekly/monthly business-review cadence with dashboard; size one GTM/customer initiative; run a cross-functional decision with stakeholder map and RACI; prepare an executive memo and 10-minute review; create a talent/org-health plan; redesign one workflow for AI with adoption and governance measures; then present a quarterly reallocation decision based on evidence.

    Proof Rubric

    Separate strategy from planning; show coherent choices and resource/financial logic; turn KPIs into operating decisions; lead cross-functional execution and influence senior stakeholders; demonstrate customer/GTM judgment, talent and organizational-health leadership; quantify business outcomes and risks; and treat AI transformation as operating-model change with adoption, governance and explicit measures rather than a technology demo.
