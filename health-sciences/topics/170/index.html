    5. Repair is incomplete until the learner re-tests after delay
    Deep explanationReading an explanation can fix understanding in the moment, but durable change is demonstrated only when the concept survives delay and appears in a new representation. The Academy uses 24-72 hour re-test plus later mixed sampling.
    Why learners miss itImmediate reattempts benefit from memory of the explanation and can create false confidence.
    Easiest clueDifferent item + later time. Recognition of the same question is not retrieval.
    6. Interleave after initial structure is built
    Deep explanationBlocked practice is efficient for first learning of a new pattern; interleaving related problems later forces the learner to decide which strategy, diagnosis or formula applies. That discrimination resembles real exams and clinical work.
    Why learners miss itLearners either mix too early and feel random, or remain blocked too long and never practice strategy selection.
    Easiest clueBuild first, mix second. Interleave when you can solve the basic form but need to choose among look-alikes.

    D. TOP 10 REVIEW CONCEPTS - #4 TO #6 — E. SPIRAL MAINTENANCE RULE

    Keep strong but high-value material alive with brief retrieval. Repair recurring errors aggressively. Re-test repaired concepts on new material. Defer low-yield duplication.
