    Treatment sequenceWhat must happen before, with or after the definitive intervention?Recognizes when stabilization, source control, monitoring, prevention or supportive care is part of the tested sequence.

    I. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED INTEGRATION CONCEPTS - #7 TO #10

    # / CONCEPTDEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    7. Initial and definitive therapy are different decisionsThe correct first action may stabilize physiology, cover dangerous possibilities or reduce immediate harm before the definitive treatment becomes appropriate.Learners choose the treatment that eventually solves the disease rather than the next action in sequence.Look for now vs later: what must happen first?
    8. Drug mechanism and toxicity belong in the same modelTherapeutic effect and predictable adverse effects often arise from the same receptor, enzyme, pathway or organ handling. Mechanism lets you infer both.Drug lists are memorized as indications separate from toxicities.For every drug ask target -> effect -> signature harm -> monitoring.
    9. Complications are downstream predictions, not aIf you understand the failed mechanism, you can anticipate what organ system, hemodynamic state, infection risk or metabolicComplications are often learned as another isolated memorization table.Ask if this failure continues, what breaks next?
