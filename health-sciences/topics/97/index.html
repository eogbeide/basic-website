    CHRONIC NEUROLOGY + PSYCHIATRY

    ShareXinf

    YEAR 2 | PAGE 26F - MOVEMENT, DEMYELINATION, NMJ, MOOD, PSYCHOSIS + PHARM

    NEUROPSYCHIATRY INTEGRATION NEURO: pattern + localization + tempo -> mechanism -> confirmatory test -> disease-modifying/symptomatic therapy. PSYCH: syndrome + duration +

    functional impairment + substances/medical causes -> safety -> diagnosis -> therapy. Medication questions require mechanism, signature adverse effect and the syndrome the drug can mimic.

    1

    Multiple Sclerosis - Ninja Nerd~54 min
    Immune-mediated CNS demyelination, optic neuritis/INO and other localizing findings, MRI/CSF diagnosis, relapse treatment and disease-modifying therapy.

    2

    Parkinson's Disease Drugs - Ninja Nerd~1 hr 37
    Basal ganglia direct/indirect pathways plus levodopa, dopamine agonists, COMT/MAO-B inhibitors, amantadine and anticholinergic mechanisms/adverse effects.

    3

    Drugs for Epilepsy - Ninja Nerd~1 hr 07
    Mechanism-first antiepileptic pharmacology, seizure-type selection, major toxicities, teratogenicity and practice questions.

    4

    Top NBME Concepts - Psychiatry (Step 1) - HyGuru~1 hr 10
    Integrated review of mood disorders, grief vs depression, mania/lithium, psychotic disorders, eating disorders and substance-use/toxicology patterns.

    5

    Anxiolytic & Hypnotic Drugs - Ninja Nerd~1 hr 09
    Anxiety/insomnia pathophysiology plus benzodiazepines, Z-drugs, barbiturates, buspirone and related safety/adverse-effect reasoning.

    6

    Dopamine Pathways, Antipsychotics + EPS - Dirty Medicinetargeted
    High-yield mesolimbic/mesocortical/nigrostriatal/tuberoinfundibular pathways; ties antipsychotic action to EPS and endocrine adverse effects.

    6 — PSYCHIATRY DIFFERENTIATION CHECK

    • Use time course + functional impairment to separate normal stress/grief from mood/anxiety disorders; screen substance/medication/medical causes before assuming primary psychiatry.
    • For psychosis, separate schizophrenia-spectrum disorders from mood disorders with psychotic features, delirium, substance-induced states and neurologic disease.
    • Know medication emergencies: serotonin syndrome, NMS, lithium toxicity, valproate/carbamazepine toxicity, benzodiazepine withdrawal and severe antipsychotic metabolic/EPS effects.
