    H. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED SYSTEM CONCEPTS - #7 TO #10

    # / CONCEPTDEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    7. Syndrome comes before organismThe same pathogen causes different syndromes and the same syndrome can come from many pathogens. Site, host and exposure define the useful prior probability before any buzzword or culture result.Alphabetical organism memorization creates recognition without diagnostic sequencing.Ask where is the infection, who is the host, and what exposure changed the odds?
    8. A diagnostic test is defined by what it detectsCulture requires viable organisms; NAAT detects nucleic acid; antigen assays detect microbial products; serology often reflects host antibody timing; microscopy depends on burden and morphology. The correct test also depends on specimen quality and timing.Learners memorize “best tests” without understanding the assay target, so they choose the wrong test or wrong timing.Ask what does this test physically detect, in which specimen, at what stage?
