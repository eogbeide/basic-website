    C. WHAT SCHOOL EXAMS COMMONLY REQUIRE — E. PATHOLOGY ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Mechanism-to-morphology gap - recognized the lesion name but could not explain why it looks that way.
    • Sequence/mediator gap - knew components but confused upstream/downstream events in injury, inflammation or repair.
    • Hemodynamic causal-chain gap - failed to connect Virchow factors, thrombus/embolus/infarct or shock compensation.
    • Neoplasia classification gap - confused oncogene vs tumor suppressor, benign vs malignant, invasion, grade or stage.
    • Image/context anchoring gap - memorized one picture and failed when the same process appeared from a new specimen/angle.

    SCHOOL-EXAM GATE

    Proceed when you can name and EXPLAIN an unfamiliar gross/microscopic change, build cause -> mechanism -> morphology -> consequence, distinguish major general-pathology processes on unseen vignettes/images, and state why plausible distractors are wrong.
