    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own an end-to-end P&C pricing and reserving case. Clean exposure/claims data in R or Python/SQL; fit frequency and severity assumptions plus a GLM or comparable model; estimate an indicated rate and reserve; validate calibration, segmentation, stability and fairness; forecast trends and stress catastrophe/emerging-risk scenarios; analyze portfolio profitability and rate adequacy; document model governance and regulatory assumptions; present pricing actions, uncertainty and tradeoffs to underwriting and finance.

    Proof Rubric

    Show actuarial evidence, not a spreadsheet answer: auditable data checks; defensible frequency/severity and reserving assumptions; correct exposure and trend treatment; GLM/model diagnostics and out-of-sample validation; calibrated rate indication; reserve/scenario uncertainty; portfolio profitability insight; fairness and model-risk controls; reproducible R/Python/SQL work; clear documentation; translate technical findings into an underwriting or leadership decision with quantified impact and limitations.
