    10. In OPP/OMM, diagnosis and safety precede technique selection Deep explanationOsteopathic treatment begins with history/red flags, tissue texture and motion findings, a named somatic dysfunction and an indication/contraindication check. Technique is chosen only after that reasoning and is followed by reassessment.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueTechnique names and setup steps are memorable, so learners may jump to treatment without proving the dysfunction or excluding risk. No diagnosis, no technique. Identify dysfunction + safety boundary first; hands-on competence requires supervised practice.
