    E. QUESTION-REVIEW ALGORITHM — F. MEDICINE ERROR TAXONOMY

    • Recognition gap - did not identify the syndrome or classic pattern.
    • Diagnostic-sequence gap - knew the disease but chose a confirmatory test before an indicated screening/initial test, or vice versa.
    • Management-sequence gap - chose a correct therapy at the wrong time or after a missed stabilization step.
    • Medication/safety gap - contraindication, adverse effect, renal/hepatic dosing, interaction or monitoring requirement.
    • Disposition/prevention gap - missed admission/ICU need, follow-up, screening, vaccination or secondary prevention.
    • Reasoning/timing gap - anchoring, ignoring a decisive clue, overtesting or running out of time.
