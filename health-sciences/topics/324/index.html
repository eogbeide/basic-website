    F. FINAL FEEDBACK + COACHING MASTERY GATE

    GatePass condition
    Goal ObservationCan name one observable behavior to improve before the encounter. Seeks direct observation when the skill can only be judged by watching/listening.
    Specificity ReceptionCan translate broad comments into behavior + effect + desired alternative. Can clarify difficult feedback without immediate rejection or global self-labeling.
    Prioritization PracticeChooses the highest-value one or two targets rather than trying to repair everything at once. Uses deliberate repetitions with attention, feedback and adjustment.
    Re-observation TransferObtains a new observed sample after the correction. Checks whether the skill persists across time, patients, settings or reduced prompting.
    Synthesis Self-monitoringUses multiple observations longitudinally while escalating credible safety concerns promptly. Can propose a gap hypothesis and practice plan before relying on the coach to solve the problem.
