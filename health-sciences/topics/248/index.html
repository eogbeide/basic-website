    H. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION CLERKSHIP STUDY DECISIONS — I. CURRENT OFFICIAL ASSESSMENT ANCHORS

    10 Clinical feedback strong but timed blocks remain poor. Preserve clinical behaviors; separately train exam representation, sequence retrieval, pacing and endurance. NBME Clinical Science Mastery Series currently offers 50-question forms across eight clinical areas; standard-paced forms allow 1 hour 15 minutes. COMAT Clinical Subject exams contain 120 items in 2.5 hours and integrate OPP/OMM within each discipline blueprint. COMAT-SE provides 60 blueprint-aligned learning-assessment questions per form with immediate explanations / progress feedback.
