    I. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION READINESS QUESTIONS

    #Scenario / decision
    1You finish the Qbank at 76%, but 20% of recent corrects are guesses and old errors recur. Are you ready because the percentage is high?
    2A self-paced NBME form is strong, but timed mixed blocks collapse late. Which readiness gate is still open?
    3Two recent official forms are similar, while one older form was much lower before remediation. Which signal should carry more weight?
    4You completed every content video but have not done unfamiliar mixed questions. Is coverage evidence enough?
    5A COMSAE score looks reassuring, but your school requires an additional internal benchmark. Which rule controls?
    6Your assessment subscores show one tiny category as weak, but the overall pattern is stable. Should that subscore alone trigger a full-course restart?
    7Your first-half accuracy is stable but the last quarter drops sharply across multiple long blocks. What should you train?
    8You know the content but have never opened the new official exam interface or planned breaks. Is this merely administrative?
    9One assessment is unexpectedly low after poor sleep and interruptions. Should you ignore it entirely?
    10You have concordant recent readiness evidence, narrow known weaknesses, stable timing, and complete logistics. What is the highest-yield final-week move?
