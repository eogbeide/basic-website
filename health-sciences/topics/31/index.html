    FOUNDATIONAL CLOSED-BOOK CHECK

    • Calculate or predict bioavailability, Vd, clearance, half-life, loading/maintenance dosing and first- vs zero-order elimination.
    • Interpret dose-response curves: potency vs efficacy, partial agonism, competitive vs noncompetitive antagonism and therapeutic index.
    • For alpha/beta/muscarinic/nicotinic receptors, state location -> G protein/second messenger -> organ effect -> agonist/antagonist consequence.
