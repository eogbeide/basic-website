    IMMUNOLOGY - HIGH-YIELD EXAM REVIEW

    YEAR 1-2 | USMLE STEP 1 + COMLEX LEVEL 1 + QBANK TRANSFER

    WHY IMMUNOLOGY GETS A DEDICATED REVIEW LAYER

    USMLE currently classifies Immunology as overlapping roughly 5-15% of Step 1 items; the Blood/Lymphoreticular/Immune system accounts for about 9-13%. The same concepts recur through infectious disease, hematology, rheumatology, pharmacology and transplant medicine.
