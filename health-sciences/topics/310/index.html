    C. THREE-AXIS PRIORITY QUEUE - NO FAKE UNIVERSAL SCORE

    AxisQuestionPriority rises when...

    Control ruleWhat evidence will let this leave the queue?A delayed fresh retest succeeds under representative conditions.
