    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Act as the Business Operations lead for a real or simulated business unit. Write a strategy kernel and explicit priorities; build an annual/quarterly operating plan, budget and headcount/resource scenarios; define a KPI tree and SQL-backed dashboard; establish weekly/monthly operating rhythms and a decision log; map one fragmented cross-functional workflow and redesign it with clear ownership, SLAs and controls; automate one repeatable step only where justified; run an executive review; then produce a decision memo, adoption plan and before/after impact readout.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate strategy-to-execution evidence: coherent priorities and stop-doing choices; auditable planning/resource assumptions; trusted metrics and SQL/data logic; useful operating cadence rather than meeting volume; measurable process improvement; explicit decision rights and accountability; scenario sensitivity; safe automation with human controls; stakeholder influence; concise executive synthesis; quantify cycle time, budget, quality, capacity or business-outcome improvement and identify where the operating system still breaks.
