    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Build a publication-ready writing portfolio for three audiences: one researched explanatory/technical article, one narrative feature or essay and one persuasive/product/UX piece. Create an audience brief, source map and fact-check log; keep version history with editor feedback and a reverse outline; test structure/clarity with at least two readers; adapt one piece into a second channel or interface; use AI for research, synthesis or revision on one asset while documenting prompts, citations, human edits, confidentiality and uncertainty checks; then deliver a style/voice guide and editorial postmortem showing what changed and why.

    Proof Rubric

    Show reader-centered judgment and editorial evidence: clear audience/purpose; precise, economical prose; trustworthy sourcing and fact-checking; compelling structure and narrative movement; persuasive reasoning without overstating evidence; meaningful structural and sentence-level revision; consistent voice across formats; accessible UX/content choices where relevant; transparent AI assistance with human verification; and a publication-ready final portfolio whose changes can be traced to feedback, evidence and editorial decisions.
