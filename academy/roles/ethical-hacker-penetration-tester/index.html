    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Plan and execute an authorized enterprise penetration-test simulation inside an isolated lab or written-scope environment. Write rules of engagement, approved assets and stop conditions; map attack surfaces and threat hypotheses; assess web/API, identity/cloud and one additional approved surface; record non-destructive proof, affected controls and ATT&CK mappings; remove test artifacts; prioritize remediation; produce technical and executive reports; then retest fixes and document defensive learning.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove legal authorization and scope discipline first; no testing beyond approved assets. Show reproducible but non-destructive evidence, least-impact validation, secrets/privacy handling, cleanup, accurate severity, ATT&CK mapping where useful, clear remediation, retest evidence, and communication that helps defenders reduce risk rather than merely demonstrating exploitation.
