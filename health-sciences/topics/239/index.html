    F. STRUCTURAL-DIAGNOSIS CLOSED-BOOK CHAIN — 7. Ease/barrier logic prevents reversed diagnosis and setup DEEP EXPLANATION Biomechanics questions often become easier when you first identify where the segment moves freely and where motion is restricted. That logic anchors the diagnosis and any direct/indirect setup. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Learners memorize diagnostic labels or hand positions separately from the motion that defines them. EASIEST CLUE When confused, stop naming the technique and state the ease and restrictive barrier first. 8. Viscerosomatic and Chapman associations are adjunct clues, not substitutes for diagnosis DEEP EXPLANATION Autonomic and Chapman maps can support an osteopathic interpretation, but they do not replace history, physical examination, standard diagnostic testing or urgent medical evaluation. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Highly memorable association maps can become over-weighted in board review. EASIEST CLUE If the stem contains a dangerous conventional diagnosis, address that first; use OPP relationships only in the appropriate sequence. 9. Manual-skill mastery requires supervised feedback DEEP EXPLANATION Palpation, positioning, force, tissue response and patient communication are psychomotor behaviors.
