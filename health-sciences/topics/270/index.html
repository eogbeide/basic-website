    C. REVIEW DEPTH BY ANSWER QUALITY — 4. Qbank percentage is not the same thing as learning quality DEEP EXPLANATION Block percentage is affected by topic selection, first-pass status, repeats, tutor versus timed mode and the mix of easy/hard questions. The more important signal is whether

    previously repaired decision rules transfer to new mixed questions under appropriate timing.

    C. REVIEW DEPTH BY ANSWER QUALITY — WHY STUDENTS MISS IT A number is emotionally salient and easy to compare, so students may chase a daily score rather than inspect how they are reasoning. EASIEST CLUE Track recurring error families and delayed transfer alongside percent correct. 5. Fragile correct answers belong in the error system DEEP EXPLANATION A lucky guess, answer-choice recognition, unexplained intuition or repeated answer switching can produce a correct item without a reproducible decision process. Step 2 requires

    transfer to a new presentation, so correctness alone can hide an unresolved gap.

    WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Most review workflows filter only wrong answers, which makes the dashboard look better than the underlying reasoning actually is. EASIEST CLUE If you cannot explain why the winner wins and the closest distractor loses, review it. 6. Focused blocks diagnose and repair; mixed timed blocks prove transfer DEEP EXPLANATION Targeted questions efficiently expose repeated patterns while you are rebuilding a weak area, but prolonged topic-filtered practice supplies a strong cue about what the answer

    should be. Mixed blocks remove that cue and test recognition, task selection and management sequencing together.

    WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Improvement in a focused set feels decisive, so learners may remain there and mistake pattern familiarity for general readiness. EASIEST CLUE Use focused -> delayed focused -> mixed. The repair is not locked until the mixed step works.
