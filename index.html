About Me

I build agentic AI and platform products that turn complexity into measurable business impact.

Manny Ogbeide is an enterprise AI product and platform leader with more than 12 years across product, technology, and engineering, including 10+ years defining AI strategies and multi-domain platform roadmaps. He builds agentic systems on foundation models (GPT-5 orchestration, fine-tuned LLMs, RAG, MCP), fraud-detection models that outperform legacy rules, and payment and monetization platforms across finance, procurement, and go-to-market. His work combines a risk and controls mindset, shaped by safety engineering, financial controls, and AI governance, with the ability to lead matrixed product, engineering, data science, and business teams, delivering more than $250M in measurable business impact.

He currently leads enterprise AI and agentic product initiatives at Microsoft, owning platform roadmaps and directing agentic capabilities spanning multi-agent architecture, foundation-model reasoning, enterprise knowledge retrieval, and workflow orchestration. Earlier, as Lead Senior Product Manager (Technical) at Amazon, he owned machine-learning forecasting and fraud-detection platforms reaching 99.8% accuracy, and before that led data and technical product management across AWS's infrastructure supply chain.

Outside Microsoft, Manny is the founder of Zuyini, an AI consulting and education practice, where he created the Zuyini Academy skills platform and authored the Zuyini Product Management Framework. He also served as President of the ASU Business Information Management Association and founded the Future Leaders Scholarship Program, funding scholarships for underserved students across Africa.

His education includes an MBA from Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business, an MSc in Process Engineering from the University of Nottingham, and executive management training from Yale School of Management, completed with Distinction.
