    F. AUTHORSHIP + PUBLICATION-INTEGRITY RULES

    IssueAcademy operating rule
    AuthorshipUse the current journal/ICMJE criteria. Authorship is credit plus accountability; contributors who do not meet authorship criteria should be credited appropriately.
    Author order + rolesDiscuss expectations early, document roles/contributions and revisit as work changes; avoid honorary or coercive authorship.
    ReferencesVerify that original sources support the exact claim; check retractions/corrections rather than copying citations from secondary or AI-generated text.
    Funding + conflictsDisclose support, relationships and roles according to journal/institution rules.
    AI-assisted workHumans remain responsible. Follow journal policy; disclose use as required; AI tools cannot be authors. Verify generated text, analysis, images and citations.
    Confidential manuscriptsDo not upload privileged/unpublished manuscripts or peer-review material to AI systems when confidentiality cannot be assured or policy does not permit it.
    Peer review/revisionRespond point-by-point; distinguish clarification, new analysis and disagreement. Never fabricate data/analysis merely to satisfy a reviewer.
