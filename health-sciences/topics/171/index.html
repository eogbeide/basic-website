    8. School and board priorities can differ without either being wrong
    Deep explanationA locally emphasized detail may be essential for the next school exam yet low-frequency on a national blueprint. Conversely, a board-relevant domain may be lightly taught locally. The review system must satisfy both on different time horizons.
    Why learners miss itStudents often force a single priority list onto two different assessments.
    Easiest clueLocal now; national longitudinally. Match emphasis to the assessment while preserving future maintenance.
    9. Compression is useful only when it makes retrieval faster
    Deep explanationA one-page map, comparison table, illness script or management ladder should expose relationships and decision points. Compression should be generated from understanding, then used as a retrieval cue - not as a decorative rewrite.
    Why learners miss itCreating polished notes feels productive even when nothing is being retrieved or tested.
    Easiest clueIf the page cannot trigger recall or a decision, it is storage, not review.
    10. Escalate resources only after diagnosing the error type
    Deep explanationKnowledge gaps may need teaching; reasoning gaps need comparison and explanation; sequence gaps need algorithms; representation gaps need images/graphs/practicals; timing gaps need timed practice. A new resource should have one defined job.
    Why learners miss itStudents respond to every miss with more content, even when the problem is reasoning, timing or sequencing.
    Easiest clueName the failure before choosing the fix.
