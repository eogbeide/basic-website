Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

Act as HR Director for a simulated 2,000-5,000 employee enterprise or multi-business portfolio. Translate business strategy into a three-year people strategy and workforce model; redesign one organization and decision/role architecture; create leadership, succession, internal-mobility and critical-talent plans; define rewards and employee-value-proposition principles; build an executive people-analytics and organizational-health scorecard; assess employee/labor and policy risks; lead a reorganization or M&A-style change scenario; define the HR operating model across HRBPs, CoEs and shared services; design the HR data/technology architecture; redesign one workflow for human-AI collaboration with privacy, fairness, accountability and escalation controls; then deliver a board-style human-capital memo and annual people-governance calendar.

Proof Rubric

Show strategic alignment between business and people choices; coherent org/workforce design; credible succession and leadership pipelines; fair, transparent talent architecture; people analytics that change executive decisions; disciplined employee/labor-risk governance; culture and rewards logic; effective transformation sequencing; scalable HR operating model and technology design; responsible human-AI work architecture; measurable productivity/experience outcomes; and executive/board communication that is concise, evidence-backed and explicit about uncertainty and tradeoffs.
