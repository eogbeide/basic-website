    System emphasis

    The official 2026 outline distributes foundational science across organ systems. High-weight combined groupings include reproductive/endocrine (12-16%), respiratory + renal/urinary (11-15%), behavioral health + nervous/special senses (10-14%), and blood/lymphoreticular/immune (9-13%).     What this means for review

    • Pathology and physiology must be continuously integrated with every system.
    • Do not leave nutrition, communication/social science, biostatistics or patient-safety concepts for the last day.
