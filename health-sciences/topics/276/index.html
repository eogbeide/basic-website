FINAL 5-7 DAYS - WHAT STAYS IN / WHAT COMES OUT — 10. Final readiness should become simpler, more stable and more reproducible as test day approaches DEEP EXPLANATION A mature final-week plan has fewer unresolved error classes, stable timed performance, familiar interface behavior and a clear test-day routine. Readiness is not the absence of

    anxiety or the memorization of every detail; it is the convergence of recent evidence with reliable execution. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Anxiety often drives learners to reopen broad resources, search for hidden high-yield lists or perform exhausting last-minute volume that increases variance rather than reducing it.
