    ANATOMY, HISTOLOGY & EMBRYOLOGY

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Build 3D structural understanding, tissue recognition and developmental reasoning that can be applied to imaging, procedures, neurologic localization and congenital disease.

    Mastery checklist

    • Anatomic terminology, planes and relationships; thorax, abdomen, pelvis/perineum, head/neck, limbs and back.
    • Neurovascular supply, dermatomes, lymphatics and clinically important spaces.
    • Histology of epithelium, connective tissue, muscle, nerve, vessels and major organs.
    • Embryologic weeks 1-8, folding, germ-layer derivatives, neurulation, cardiac/gut/urogenital development and congenital anomalies.
    • Cross-sectional anatomy and basic CT/MRI/radiograph orientation.

    You should be able to...

    • Localize a lesion from anatomy and symptoms.
    • Predict deficits from nerve, vessel or compartment injury.
    • Recognize major tissues and correlate microscopic structure with function.
    • Explain congenital anomalies from disrupted developmental steps.
    • Translate anatomy into physical-exam and procedural landmarks.

    High-value learning resources — Exam alignment

    • USMLE Step 1 explicitly includes gross anatomy/embryology (10-20% discipline overlap) and histology/cell biology (5-15% overlap).
    • COMLEX Level 1 integrates anatomy within clinical presentations and OPP/OMM.
    • School anatomy practicals require image/cadaver/structure identification beyond board-question practice.

    High-value learning resources — Mastery / assessment loop

    • After each region: closed-book structure sketch -> practical images -> 20-40 board-style questions.
    • Maintain an "anatomy errors" deck containing only relationships you actually miss.
    • Before a practical: mixed stations + radiology cross-sections + timed identification.
