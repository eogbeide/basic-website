    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Lead AI adoption for one real or simulated business function: baseline the workflow, stakeholder readiness and adoption barriers; activate an executive sponsor and champion network; redesign the future-state process and human/AI handoffs; segment user personas; build role-based communication and enablement; define responsible-AI, security and escalation controls; instrument usage, task success, cycle time, quality and employee/customer outcomes; run a pilot and feedback loop; then make a scale/hold/sunset recommendation with governance, owners and a 30/60/90-day sustainment plan.

    Proof Rubric

    Show behavioral and business evidence, not attendance: readiness baseline; sponsor/coalition activation; validated future-state workflow; role-based enablement; measurable usage, task success and value; corrective action from feedback; responsible-AI controls and human escalation; governance/ownership; explicit scale criteria; executive narrative; and proof that observed behavior changed the rollout plan.
