    YEAR 2 SYSTEM BLOCKS

    MSK / RHEUM / DERM — Core checklist

    • Bone/joint/muscle physiology and pathology.
    • Inflammatory vs degenerative arthritis, connective-tissue disease and vasculitis.
    • Bone tumors/metabolic bone disease.
    • Dermatologic morphology, inflammatory disease, infection and malignancy.

    MSK / RHEUM / DERM — Mastery outcomes

    • Differentiate inflammatory from mechanical patterns.
    • Recognize common rash morphology and dangerous presentations.
    • Connect autoantibodies/pathophysiology to rheumatic disease. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    NEUROLOGY / PSYCHIATRY — Core checklist

    • Neuroanatomic localization and vascular syndromes.
    • Seizure, headache, movement, demyelinating, neuromuscular and neurodegenerative disease.
    • Mood, anxiety, psychotic, substance-use, personality and neurocognitive disorders.
    • Neurologic/psychiatric pharmacology and adverse effects.

    NEUROLOGY / PSYCHIATRY — Mastery outcomes

    • Localize before diagnosing.
    • Choose first-line diagnostic and management steps in common syndromes.
    • Differentiate medication adverse effects from disease progression. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    SYSTEM BLOCK ASSESSMENT

    Complete a targeted question set after each major topic, then a timed mixed block at the end of the system. Remediate by mechanism, not by memorizing the question.
