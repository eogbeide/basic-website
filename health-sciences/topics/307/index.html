    D. RESPONSIBLE-USE BOUNDARY MAP

    SituationSafe defaultEscalate / stop when
    Personal study with public/synthetic contentUse AI for tutoring, retrieval, critique and organization; verify substantive claims.The task becomes graded, policy-restricted or requires confidential material.
    Graded assignment / exam / take-home workFollow explicit course rules; disclose use if required; preserve your own reasoning and authorship.AI use is prohibited, ambiguous or would replace the assessed competency.
    Clinical vignette practiceUse synthetic/de-identified educational cases.Real patient identifiers, unique combinations or institutional confidential data would be entered.
    Patient-care decision supportUse only institution-approved clinical systems within supervision and policy.A general consumer AI tool would influence real care without approved governance.
    Clinical note / handoff practicePractice on synthetic cases; compare against faculty rubric.Real PHI is involved or the output could enter the legal medical record without approved workflow.
    Sensitive learner/admissions dataUse only systems permitted by the institution and applicable privacy rules.Personal web AI would receive confidential learner/applicant data.
