    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Choose a research question with a falsifiable hypothesis. Reproduce a credible baseline; define metrics and statistical tests; build a controlled experiment with ablations; track data and code versions; analyze failures and uncertainty; then implement one meaningful model, data, post-training or inference improvement. Produce a research report with methods, results, limitations, negative findings, compute/cost and next experiments, plus a reproducible artifact.

    Proof Rubric

    Show a clear hypothesis and literature-grounded rationale; reproducible code/data; valid baselines and controls; sound statistics; honest uncertainty and negative results; meaningful ablations; evaluation robustness; compute and cost awareness; evidence that the improvement generalizes; and a concise research narrative separating observation, inference and speculation. Novelty matters only when supported by trustworthy evidence.
