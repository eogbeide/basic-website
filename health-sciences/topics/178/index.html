    Easiest clueWho makes it -> who receives it -> what changes? That triad is more durable than a list.
    5. Antigen location determines MHC pathway and T-cell response Deep explanationEndogenous/cytosolic antigen is generally presented on MHC I to CD8 cells; exogenous/phagocytosed antigen is generally presented on MHC II to CD4 cells. This connects infection type to effector response.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueMHC numbers and CD numbers are memorized as pairings without understanding why the pairing exists. Inside the cell -> I -> CD8; outside/phagocytosed -> II -> CD4. Start with antigen location.
    6. Immunodeficiency is an infection-pattern localization problem Deep explanationRecurrent organism class, age of onset, absent tissue structures, vaccine reactions and basic blood-cell/immunoglobulin data localize the missing immune arm before the syndrome name.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueClassic eponyms dominate memory and fail when the vignette omits the hallmark association. Organism pattern -> immune function -> syndrome. Localize the missing defense first.
