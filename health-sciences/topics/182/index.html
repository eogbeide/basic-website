    Easiest clueForce a one-line localization first: “This is a left cortical / right brainstem / cord / peripheral pattern because...”
    5. Decussation determines the side of the deficit Deep explanationTracts cross at different levels. A lesion above versus below the crossing changes whether deficits are ipsilateral or contralateral. Brainstem lesions may combine ipsilateral cranial-nerve findings with contralateral body deficits.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueStudents memorize “contralateral” globally instead of tracing the pathway. Draw origin -> crossing -> target. Place the lesion on that route before deciding side.
    6. Behavioral and psychiatric patterns require duration, impairment and context Deep explanationSymptoms alone are insufficient. Diagnostic patterns depend on how long they persist, whether they impair function, developmental stage, substances/medications, medical causes and whether the response is proportionate to context.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueLearners recognize symptom words but ignore timing and function discriminators. Duration + function + context should be checked before assigning a psychiatric label.
