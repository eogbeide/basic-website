    COACHING RULE: A COACH SHOULD HELP THE LEARNER SEE PERFORMANCE DATA, DEFINE A TARGET, DESIGN PRACTICE AND BUILD SELF-MONITORING. DELIBERATE PRACTICE IS NOT MERE REPETITION; EACH REP HAS A PURPOSE, FEEDBACK AND A STANDARD FOR IMPROVEMENT.

    C. ROLE CLARITY: WHO IS DOING WHAT?

    RolePrimary jobBest learner use
    Coach Clinical teacherFacilitates reflection, goal setting, pattern recognition, practice design and accountability. Teaches content/skills and may coach in the moment during patient care.Learner owns the plan and becomes better at self-monitoring. Instruction + observed correction in context.
    Evaluator AdvisorJudges performance against a standard and contributes assessment data. Provides program, career, scheduling or policy guidance.Credible evidence for progression decisions. Better choices within institutional constraints.
    MentorOffers longitudinal perspective, sponsorship and professional guidance.Career/professional growth; not necessarily direct performance coaching.

    D. DELIBERATE-PRACTICE LOOP

    Loop stepActionProof
    1. Baseline 2. IsolateObserve the current performance under realistic conditions. Break the complex task into the smallest high-leverage subskill.Know the actual gap. A practice target small enough to change.
    3. Define standard 4. AttemptState what competent performance looks/sounds like. Perform with full attention under safe supervision/simulation.A visible success criterion. One authentic repetition.
    5. Feedback 6. RetryReceive immediate, behavior-specific information. Repeat promptly with the correction.Know what to preserve and change. Demonstrate that the adjustment is executable.
    7. Increase difficulty 8. Delay + transferAdd time pressure, complexity, different patients or fewer prompts. Recheck later in a fresh case/setting.Avoid context-bound mastery. Retention + generalization.
