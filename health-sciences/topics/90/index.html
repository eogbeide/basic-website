    C. WHAT SCHOOL EXAMS COMMONLY REQUIRE

    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Syndrome localizationPneumonia, meningitis/encephalitis, UTI, endocarditis, skin/soft-tissue, GI infection, STI and fever without a clear source.
    Host + exposureAge, pregnancy, immune status, devices, hospital exposure, travel, animals/food/water, sexual exposure and vaccination history.
    Specimen + diagnosticsBlood/urine/CSF/sputum/wound samples; Gram stain/culture; NAAT/PCR; antigen/serology; imaging; test timing before antibiotics when safe.
    Empiric -> definitiveCover dangerous likely pathogens first, then narrow to culture/susceptibility or molecular result; account for site penetration and allergies.
    Resistance + stewardshipBeta-lactamases, altered targets, efflux/permeability and biofilm; antibiogram logic; source control; de-escalation and duration principles.
    HIV + opportunistic infectionHIV testing/viral load/CD4 logic; common OIs by immune status; ART classes/interactions; prophylaxis and immune-reconstitution concepts.
    Vaccines + infection controlStandard precautions plus contact/droplet/airborne isolation; vaccine-preventable disease; exposure/post-exposure concepts and reporting.
    Sepsis + public healthRecognize unstable infection, source control and cultures without delaying urgent care; know outbreak, exposure and prevention reasoning.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE

    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Days 1-2Respiratory/CNS/urinary/cardiac syndromes. Build pathogen lists from site + host + exposure, then choose the first diagnostic test.
    Days 3-4Antibiotics + resistance. Match syndrome to empiric coverage; practice narrowing from cultures, susceptibility and patient factors.
    Day 5HIV/OIs, TB, fungi, viruses, STIs and C. difficile. Use immune status, transmission and prevention to separate look-alikes.
    Day 6Vaccines/isolation/stewardship + mixed timed block. Force yourself to state why each wrong option fails by site, test or spectrum.
    Day 7Re-test every miss on new cases; rewrite only the recurring organism-drug-test associations that remain unstable.
