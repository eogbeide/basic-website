    C. WHAT SCHOOL EXAMS COMMONLY REQUIRE

    DomainWhat mastery looks like
    LocalizationCortex vs brainstem vs cord vs peripheral nerve/NMJ/muscle; side, tract/territory and expected associated findings before disease naming.
    Stroke / acute neuroVascular territory, ischemic vs hemorrhagic pattern, imaging sequence, thrombolysis/thrombectomy concepts, hemorrhage/herniation emergencies.
    Seizure / headacheFocal vs generalized semiology, provoked vs unprovoked, status; primary headache vs SAH/meningitis/mass/ICP and first diagnostic step.
    Movement / demyelinationParkinson vs essential tremor vs Huntington; MS vs peripheral demyelination; basal ganglia pathways and characteristic treatment toxicities.
    NMJ / neuromuscularMG vs Lambert-Eaton vs botulism; neuropathy vs myopathy; reflexes, sensation, fatigability, autonomic clues and respiratory-risk recognition.
    NeurodegenerationAlzheimer, Lewy body, frontotemporal, vascular dementia, prion disease; tempo, cognitive domain, movement/behavioral clues and pathology.
    Mood / anxiety / psychosisDuration criteria, impairment, safety assessment, medical/substance exclusions, psychosis-mood relationship and first-line mechanism-based treatment.
    Psych / neuro pharmacologyAEDs, Parkinson drugs, migraine agents, antidepressants, antipsychotics, lithium/mood stabilizers, anxiolytics; mechanism + signature toxicity + dangerous interactions.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE

    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Days 1-2Localization + stroke. Draw tracts/vascular territories from memory; solve deficit-to-lesion cases before reviewing disease names.
    Days 3-4Seizures/headache + demyelination/NMJ/movement. Pair each syndrome with the decisive test and drug mechanism/toxicity.
    Day 5Psychiatric timelines: depression/bipolar/psychosis/anxiety/substance/personality/neurocognitive. Force explicit duration + exclusion logic.
    Day 6Timed mixed block. Include neuro images/CT-MRI, EEG-style descriptions, pharmacology and psychiatry vignettes; explain every distractor.
    Day 7Re-test every miss on NEW cases. Redraw only the unstable localization maps, psych timelines and drug-adverse-effect pairs.
