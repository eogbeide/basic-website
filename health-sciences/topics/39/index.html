    Error log taxonomy

    • Knowledge gap: never learned or forgot the fact/mechanism.
    • Reasoning gap: knew facts but misinterpreted the vignette.
    • Question-reading gap: missed the task/lead-in or ignored a key detail.
    • Management-sequence gap: chose a reasonable action but not the best next step.
    • Overthinking: changed from the most likely/common answer without adequate evidence.
    • Time-pressure gap: slow interpretation or calculations.
