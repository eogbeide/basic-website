    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own an enterprise technical sales cycle end to end: conduct discovery and document business/technical requirements and decision criteria; produce a target architecture with API/integration, data, identity and security views; build a tailored demo and proof-of-value plan with measurable success criteria; prototype one integration/AI workflow and evaluate it; quantify business value/ROI; run a technical workshop and executive readout; create the mutual action plan, risk/production-readiness checklist and handoff package for implementation/adoption.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate discovery depth and requirement traceability; defend architecture and integration tradeoffs; prove the solution with a working demo/POV and explicit success criteria; address identity, security, reliability and compliance; quantify customer value rather than feature activity; influence technical and executive stakeholders; cultivate champions; surface risks early; capture product feedback; and show a credible path from technical win to production adoption.
