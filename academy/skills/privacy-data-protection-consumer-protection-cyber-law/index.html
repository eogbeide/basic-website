Hands-On Mastery Capstone

Create a privacy and data-protection assessment for a digital product or business process. Trace personal-data flows, identify legal/security risks, define retention and access controls, review notices/consent and design incident plus vendor controls.

Proof Rubric

  • data-flow map
  • data inventory
  • privacy-risk assessment
  • notice/consent review
  • retention/access rules
  • vendor clauses/checklist
  • incident decision tree
  • remediation roadmap
