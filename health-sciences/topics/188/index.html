    M. EXAM-DAY QUESTION / STATION ALGORITHM — N. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED CONCEPT - #10 10. Tapering protects performance; it is not lost study time Deep explanation

    Late in the week, the main goal shifts from building new knowledge to making mastered knowledge reliably accessible under the test conditions. Short retrieval, narrow repair, sleep and logistics protect attention, working memory, pacing and error control.

    P. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION EXAM-WEEK DECISIONS — Q. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM MASTERY GATE

    - Every stated objective has an observable retrieval/performance check, and no high-frequency RED objective remains unaddressed. - At least one timed mixed set has been completed and reviewed; recurring errors are narrowing rather than repeating unchanged. - Image/practical/calculation/skills modalities have been rehearsed when the school assesses them, not replaced by text-only study. - D-1 is a taper: short retrieval, narrow repair, logistics and protected sleep; the exam-day routine is already familiar. - After recovery, useful errors become cumulative-review inputs so the next block starts with better process data.
