    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Run finance for a simulated growth-stage company for one quarter. Execute close and consolidation; issue financial statements; write a technical-accounting memo for one complex transaction; design ICFR/SOX controls and an audit PBC package; build a driver-based budget, rolling forecast and board KPI pack; model cash, liquidity and treasury stress scenarios; evaluate pricing/capital allocation plus one acquisition or strategic investment; document tax/risk assumptions; deliver a board/investor finance narrative; then modernize one finance workflow with governed automation or AI, including source checks, approvals, audit trail, exception handling and rollback. Finish with a CFO decision memo.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate books and reporting integrity; defensible GAAP conclusions; effective ICFR/SOX and audit evidence; forecast/scenario discipline; cash and liquidity control; capital-allocation and valuation judgment; transaction/financing logic; board-ready narrative; finance-system and data integrity; AI automation with human approvals and auditability; explicit risk escalation; and executive judgment that connects financial stewardship to sustainable growth.
