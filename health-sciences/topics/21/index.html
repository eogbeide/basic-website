    OSTEOPATHIC PRINCIPLES & MANIPULATIVE MEDICINE (DO TRACK)

    YEAR 1-2 LONGITUDINAL | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Build the osteopathic-specific competency layer required for DO school performance, COMLEX and COMAT, while keeping structural diagnosis and treatment clinically grounded.

    Mastery checklist

    • Osteopathic philosophy and tenets.
    • Structural examination and somatic dysfunction.
    • Biomechanics of spine, ribs, pelvis and extremities.
    • Muscle energy, HVLA, counterstrain, myofascial release and other foundational techniques as taught by the school.
    • Autonomic relationships and viscerosomatic concepts.
    • Contraindications, safety and integration with conventional diagnosis/treatment.

    You should be able to...

    • Perform a supervised structural exam and document findings.
    • Diagnose common somatic dysfunction patterns using school-taught models.
    • Choose a reasonable OMT technique and recognize contraindications.
    • Integrate OPP/OMM with a clinical vignette rather than treating it as isolated memorization.
