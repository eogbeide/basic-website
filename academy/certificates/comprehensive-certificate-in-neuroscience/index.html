Integrative Capstone

Design a neuroscience investigation around a neural circuit or cognitive function. Build a multi-level model from cells to circuits to behavior; formulate a falsifiable hypothesis; select measurement/manipulation methods; specify controls; analyze a simulated or public neural/behavioral dataset; compare alternative explanations; and produce a research report plus a follow-up experiment and translational implications section.

Evidence Standard

Mechanistic accuracy; neuroanatomical coherence; appropriate method choice; temporal/spatial resolution awareness; controls; quantitative reasoning; causal restraint; reproducibility; integration across cellular, circuit and behavioral levels; explicit uncertainty and ethical limitations.
