Integrative Capstone

Design and defend a simulated diagnostic workup from specimen receipt to final report. Define specimen requirements; select chemistry, hematology, microbiology/immunology and molecular methods; create QC and calibration logic; identify preanalytic and analytic failure modes; interpret discordant results; document reference ranges and uncertainty; build a small laboratory information workflow; and produce a validation/QMS package plus an integrated diagnostic correlation memo.

Evidence Standard

  • Specimen control
  • method appropriateness
  • QC/calibration reasoning
  • morphology and diagnostic logic
  • molecular contamination controls
  • uncertainty and interferences
  • traceability
  • CLIA/QMS awareness
  • informatics
  • safe communication
