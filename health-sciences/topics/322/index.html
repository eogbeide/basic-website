    RECEPTION RULE: RECEIVE FEEDBACK AS DATA TO INTERROGATE, NOT AN IDENTITY STATEMENT TO ACCEPT OR REJECT WHOLESALE. CLARIFY THE OBSERVATION, EXTRACT THE ACTIONABLE PART, TRIANGULATE WHEN NEEDED AND SCHEDULE A NEW REPETITION.
