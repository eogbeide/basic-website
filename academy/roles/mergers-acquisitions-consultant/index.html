    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Run a buy-side acquisition case end to end: define strategic rationale and target criteria; screen a market and shortlist targets; build standalone DCF/comps and acquisition valuation with control/synergy scenarios; conduct commercial, operational and technology diligence; quantify value-creation levers; design IMO governance, Day 1/100 and carve-out/TSA plans where relevant; develop negotiation positions and key deal terms; then deliver an IC/board memo with risks, sensitivities, disconfirming evidence and a go/no-go recommendation.

    Proof Rubric

    Show a traceable thesis and target logic; auditable valuation and scenario/sensitivity work; clear separation of standalone value, control and synergy; evidence-based diligence across market, operations and technology; explicit red-flag escalation; quantified value-creation levers; executable integration/Day 1 mechanics; disciplined negotiation logic; board-ready synthesis; and a recommendation that states what evidence would change the decision.
