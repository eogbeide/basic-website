    YEAR 2 SYSTEM BLOCKS

    ShareXinf

    CARDIOVASCULAR — Core checklist

    • Cardiac anatomy + conduction; pressure-volume loops; preload/afterload/contractility.
    • Ischemic disease, heart failure, valves, cardiomyopathy, arrhythmias, congenital lesions, hypertension and vascular disease.
    • ECG foundations, murmurs/hemodynamics, cardiac imaging concepts.
    • Antianginal, antihypertensive, antiarrhythmic, HF and anticoagulant pharmacology.

    CARDIOVASCULAR — Mastery outcomes

    • Explain murmurs and hemodynamic changes.
    • Interpret common ECG patterns at a foundational level.
    • Select diagnostic next steps and mechanism-based therapy in vignettes. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    RESPIRATORY — Core checklist

    • Ventilation/perfusion, diffusion, compliance, oxygen transport and acid-base.
    • Asthma/COPD, restrictive disease, pneumonia, pleural disease, pulmonary hypertension/embolism, ARDS, lung cancer.
    • PFTs, ABGs, imaging patterns.
    • Bronchodilators, steroids, antibiotics and pulmonary vascular therapies.

    RESPIRATORY — Mastery outcomes

    • Interpret PFT/ABG patterns.
    • Use physiology to distinguish hypoxemia mechanisms.
    • Connect respiratory drugs to disease mechanism and adverse effects. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    SYSTEM BLOCK ASSESSMENT

    Complete a targeted question set after each major topic, then a timed mixed block at the end of the system. Remediate by mechanism, not by memorizing the question.
