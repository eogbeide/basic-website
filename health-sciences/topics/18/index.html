    NEUROSCIENCE + BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Build localization, neurophysiology and behavioral foundations that support neurology, psychiatry and sensory-system disease.

    Mastery checklist

    • Neuroanatomy: tracts, brainstem, cortex, cerebellum, basal ganglia, spinal cord and vascular territories.
    • Neurophysiology, neurotransmitters and synaptic signaling.
    • Special senses.
    • Motor and sensory localization.
    • Sleep, cognition, learning, development and behavior.
    • Psychiatric diagnostic foundations and behavioral science principles.

    You should be able to...

    • Localize lesions from symptoms and exam findings.
    • Predict deficits from tract/vascular lesions.
    • Connect neurotransmitters to medications and disease.
    • Distinguish basic psychiatric syndromes.
    • Perform and interpret a focused neurologic/mental-status exam.
