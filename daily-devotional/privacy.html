Zuyini App Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Zuyini app on iPhone, Android and the web (app.zuyini.com): daily words, languages, business frameworks, relationships, fitness, interview prep, assistants, Local Finder, Community and Couples. The Zuyini Academy website (zuyini.com) has its own policy.

The Short Version

No sign-up, no ads, no selling your data.

You don't need an email or password. The app creates an anonymous account for your device, made of a random code stored on the device, so your Zuyini Tokens work and nobody can reset them. Most of your progress stays on your device. Things you choose to share, like community posts or answers for your partner, are stored on our server so others can see them. Text you send to an AI feature goes to OpenAI to get an answer. We don't run ads, we don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

What Stays On Your Device

Only on your phone or browser.

Progress, streaks and saved items

Lessons you've finished, your Daily Mission and streak, Smart Review cards, saved places and lists, alerts, and your content library are stored in the app's local storage on your device.

Journals and single-phone reflections

Reflections you write in the Couples Journal on one shared phone, and other journal entries, stay on your device.

Assistant chats and memory

Your assistant conversations and the short facts an assistant remembers about you, such as your goals, are kept on your device. They're sent to our server only with each new message, so the assistant can reply.

Speech practice recordings

Recordings you make in Before & After, and your clarity and pace scores, are saved only on your device so you can compare them over 30 days. We never receive them. Starting over in the app, or clearing the app's data, deletes them.

Reminders

Phone reminders are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. On the web, reminders are added to your own calendar.

What We Store On Our Server

Only what a feature needs to work.

Our server runs on Render, and our database is hosted by Supabase in the United States.

Your anonymous account and tokens

A random account ID, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your device's account code, your token balance and a history of token activity, such as “Translation, -1” or “Daily mission, +5”. This history never includes the content of what you asked.

Usage counts

Which days the app is used, and which sections (for example “language” or “assistant”), at most once per section per day, tied to the anonymous account. We use this only to see whether people come back and which features help, and never for ads. It includes nothing you type.

Community (only if you join)

Your nickname and avatar, the posts and likes you share, friends, Study Circles, reports and blocks. Other members see your nickname and posts. You can delete your community account in the app at any time, which deletes these.

Couples Together (only if you link)

The first names you and your partner enter, the day's question, and your answers. Your answers are shown only to your linked partner, and only after you've both answered that day. Unlinking deletes your answers from our server.

Purchases

Whether you have Pro or bought tokens, and the reference numbers our payment providers give us. We never see or store your card details.

What's Sent To Other Services

To do the thing you asked for.

OpenAI (AI features)

When you use an assistant, the translator, Local Finder, the date coach or a “Generate” button, our server sends your message, or the topic you picked, to OpenAI to create the answer. When you tap Listen, the text on screen is sent to OpenAI to make the audio. OpenAI doesn't use API content to train its models by default. Please don't put sensitive personal details in AI messages.

Your browser's speech recognition (Before & After)

To score clarity, the web app uses the speech recognition built into your browser while you read a passage aloud. Chrome and Edge send that audio to Google or Microsoft to transcribe it, under their own policies; Safari may do it on your device. Zuyini doesn't receive the audio or the transcript. If you'd rather not use it, the session still measures your pace.

Map services (Local Finder)

Your search, and your approximate location if you allow it, or the city you type, go to our server and to free OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, Photon by Komoot and Nominatim) to find places. Buttons that open Google Maps, Apple Maps, Booking.com, Airbnb, Google Flights or Skyscanner take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

Apple, Google and RevenueCat (phone purchases)

Subscriptions and token packs bought in the phone app go through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat manages them for us and receives your purchase details and your anonymous account ID.

Stripe (web purchases)

Purchases on app.zuyini.com go through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects your payment details and email under its own privacy policy, and tells us only whether the payment succeeded.

Moving To Another Device

Your account code.

The Tokens screen shows your account code. Entering it on another device moves your tokens and any Pro bought on the web. Keep it private, because anyone with the code can use your tokens.

Deleting Your Data

You're in control.

On your device

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything stored on the device, including your account code.

Community and Couples

Delete your community account from the Community screen, and unlink from Couples Together to delete your answers.

Everything else on our server

Email manny.ogbeide@gmail.com with your account code from the Tokens screen, and we'll delete your account, token history and usage counts.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

The app is for general audiences and isn't designed for or directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has shared information with us, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes To This Policy

We'll update the date at the top.

If we change what's stored or shared, we'll update this page and the date above. This is a plain-language policy for a small app, and it isn't legal advice.
