Who Actually Issues This

Every credential here is awarded by the provider you see next to it, under that provider's own rules — not by Zuyini. We don't issue anything in this section; we just find it, verify it's real, and show you exactly what you'd be earning.

Credential Taxonomy — Named What Each Provider Actually Calls It

Provider Certification

$0 to earn

The provider calls this a certification in their own words, and you'll need to pass an exam or assessment to earn it.

Verified Micro-Credential

Provider-issued

A focused, hands-on skill the provider verifies directly — through a lab assessment, a skill badge or a superbadge.

University Course Certificate

Not a degree

A certificate from the university or course team for completing the coursework or assessment. It's real, but it isn't a degree.

Completion Certificate / Badge

Not a certification

The provider's record that you finished the course or hit a milestone — useful proof, but not a certification.

Worth Knowing

Google's Professional Agentic Architect Certification exam itself isn't free — it's currently $120 at the discounted beta rate, down from $200. What is free is the Google Skills Starter tier: 35 credits a month, limited hands-on labs, and skill badges. That's the free layer listed below; the paid exam isn't part of our $0 list.

On your resume or LinkedIn, use the credential's exact name — for example “Microsoft Applied Skills: Generate reports with AI research agents,” not “Microsoft Certified AI Engineer.” The same goes for an OpenLearn Statement of Participation, a Kaggle completion certificate, an AWS badge or a Salesforce Superbadge: list it as what it is, not as a professional certification.

How We Verify Every Listing

  1. We link to each provider's own official page, not a reseller or aggregator.
  2. We confirm the credential itself is free, not just the training that leads up to it.
  3. We label every credential by its real type — certification, micro-credential, course certificate or badge — and never blur the four.
  4. We flag hidden costs up front: subscriptions, lab credits, paid product tiers or exam fees.
  5. We re-check every link and price before each update to this directory.
  6. We use the provider's exact credential name — never a rebranded or embellished version.
  7. We pair every credential with real hands-on proof, so this stays skills-first, not badge-first.
