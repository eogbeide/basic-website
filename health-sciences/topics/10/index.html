    CURRENT USMLE EMPHASIS

    Step 1 lists Gross Anatomy & Embryology at 10-20% discipline overlap and Histology & Cell Biology at 5-15%. USMLE also explicitly tests interpretation of gross and microscopic specimens. Board study should be clinically integrated, not a second anatomy course.
