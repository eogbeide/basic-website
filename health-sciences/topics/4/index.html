    Assessment ladder

    • Preclinical: school exams -> mixed board-style blocks -> NBME CBSSA / official sample experience -> Step 1.
    • DO preclinical: school exams + OMM/OPP -> COMLEX-focused bank + NBOME practice -> Level 1; USMLE Step 1 can be layered when desired.
    • Clerkships: daily patient learning + Qbank -> NBME CSMS or COMAT-SE -> school shelf/COMAT -> cumulative Step 2/Level 2 prep.
    • Advanced clinical/residency: independent-management reasoning -> Step 3 CCS / COMLEX Level 3 CDM cases.
