    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Design, automate and operate a production-style hybrid network: create an IPv4/IPv6 addressing and subnet plan; build VLAN/VRF segmentation and OSPF/BGP routing; model a small EVPN/VXLAN or equivalent fabric; connect a cloud VPC/VNet through redundant hybrid paths; implement ACL/firewall and Zero Trust controls; automate repeatable configuration and validation with Python/Ansible/IaC; instrument flow, latency, loss and route health; inject a routing or connectivity failure, diagnose it from telemetry and packet evidence, restore service, write the postmortem and produce a scalable network design/runbook package.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove packet and routing fluency; defend protocol, addressing, topology and redundancy choices; demonstrate idempotent automation and safe change validation; show cloud/hybrid and segmentation controls; quantify availability, latency, convergence and capacity tradeoffs; troubleshoot from packet/route/telemetry evidence; execute an incident with containment and recovery; document architecture, rollback, operational procedures and lessons learned clearly enough for another engineer to operate the network.
