TOP 10 MOST-MISSED EXAM-WEEK CONCEPTS - #1 TO #3 1. School objectives define scope; your notes do not define mastery Deep explanation

    Easiest clueTurn every objective into a verb. If the verb is identify, calculate, explain or perform, your review must make you do that action.
    2. Confidence must be earned by retrieval, not by recognition Deep explanationFamiliar material can feel mastered because the answer is visible in the notes. Exam readiness requires producing the explanation, pathway, image identification or next step before seeing the cue.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueRepeated exposure creates fluency and a false sense of certainty, especially late in the week. Close the source first. If you cannot produce it from a blank page or new prompt, it is not GREEN yet.

Match the study representation to the assessment representation Deep explanationWritten recall, cadaver/histology identification, calculations, OSCE behaviors and OMM practicals are different performance tasks. Knowledge can be present while performance fails because it was never rehearsed in the tested format.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueText questions feel efficient and are easier to schedule, so learners may underpractice images, spoken synthesis or hands-on sequences.
