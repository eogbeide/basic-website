    10. The final week protects execution more than it expands knowledge

    DEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    Final-week value comes from recurring-error retrieval, high-frequency images/formulas, ethics/patient-safety frameworks, official interface familiarity and stable sleep. Large new resources add cognitive load and create untested knowledge.Anxiety makes “doing more” feel safer than consolidating what is already known.If a resource cannot be learned, applied and retested before the exam, it is probably too late to add broadly.

    J. REPEATABLE DAILY OPERATING SYSTEM

    BLOCKEXECUTION
    1. Timed transfer1-2 mixed Qbank blocks/sets depending on stage and endurance. Qbank set size can differ from the current official exam block; separately rehearse current official timing/interface.
    2. Deep reviewReview incorrect + guessed + slow/fragile corrects. Explain the right answer, strongest distractor and mechanism.
    3. Targeted repair60-120 minutes only for named gaps using the existing content stack; stop when you can reconstruct the mechanism.
    4. Delayed retrievalRe-test prior errors, formulas, images and mechanisms on new questions/prompts rather than rereading notes.
    5. RecoveryProtect sleep, meals, exercise/movement, planned breaks and a stop time that supports next-day accuracy.

    K. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION SCHEDULING DECISIONS

    #SCENARIOBEST SCHEDULING RESPONSE
    1Baseline low across many systems.Reduce random volume briefly; rebuild 2-3 highest-impact mechanisms, then restore mixed work.
    2Cardio strong; 6 hours of cardio video remain.Skip broad rewatch; maintain cardio through mixed questions.
    3A renal acid-base miss recurs after prior repair.Deep mechanism repair -> blank-page reconstruction -> delayed new problem.
    4Week 3 mixed score improves but guessed corrects remain high.Review fragile corrects; do not let headline percentage hide unstable reasoning.
    5Second assessment improves total but neuro remains weak.Keep mixed work; add a focused neuro repair lane with delayed retesting.
    6Two assessments conflict sharply.Inspect domain mix/timing/fatigue; seek another planned data point and school guidance rather than panic-reset.
    7Week 5 study days are very long but late-block accuracy falls.Train pacing/break/endurance; reduce low-yield passive hours.
    8Final week starts with a new comprehensive course available.Do not begin broad new content; use error sheets, targeted repair and official interface practice.
    9Timing is poor but knowledge is stable.Use timed official-like segments and commit/move checkpoints rather than another content review.
    10Recent evidence is concordant and weak list is small.Consolidate, taper volume appropriately and protect sleep/execution.
