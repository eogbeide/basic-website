    YEAR 3 | PAGE 33C - OFFICIAL SELF-ASSESSMENTS + 14-DAY / 7-DAY / 48-HOUR FINISH

    Use official-style assessments as diagnostic instruments, not as trophies. One score is noisy; repeated domain patterns, time use, question-level reasoning and the ability to repair errors are more actionable. Your school's grading policy and exam instructions remain the source of truth.

    ToolCurrent official signalBest use

    J. THE FINAL 14 DAYS - ACADEMY READINESS SEQUENCE

    WindowExecution
    14-10 daysTake the first formal readiness check if it will leave time to act on the result. Map misses by domain + error type. Repair only the largest 2-3 gaps; keep daily rotation questions going.
    9-7 daysRun timed blocks in the exam's dominant reasoning style: diagnosis / next test / management / prevention / disposition. Re-test repaired topics with NEW items.
    7-4 daysUse a second official-style check if available and appropriate. Compare patterns across assessments rather than obsessing over a single unusual question. Tighten pacing.
    3-2 daysMixed timed work + highest-yield algorithms + medication/safety + prevention/disposition + error-sheet retrieval. Do not restart entire textbooks or video libraries.
    Final 48 hoursLight retrieval, logistics, current exam software/tutorial familiarity, sleep and nutrition. Stop heavy new learning early enough to preserve cognition and endurance.

    K. READINESS GATE - SIX SIGNALS

    • Coverage: all blueprint domains have been sampled; no major content area was accidentally skipped because you never saw it clinically.
    • Trend: timed Qbank and official-style performance is stable or improving across several sets, not dependent on one score.
    • Error stability: recurring knowledge and sequence errors are shrinking; the same mistake is not appearing every week.
    • Timing: you can finish appropriately sized timed blocks without a late-block collapse in accuracy.
    • Reasoning: you can explain why the next step comes before the definitive step and why the closest distractor is wrong.
    • Clinical safety: you identify instability, medication contraindications, escalation needs and disposition red flags reliably.

    K. READINESS GATE - SIX SIGNALS — L. INTERFACE / TEST-DAY PRACTICE

Use the official sample assessment/tutorial to practice navigation, lab search, highlighting, image controls and notes. For the actual shelf/COMAT, follow your school's secure-testing instructions and accommodations.
