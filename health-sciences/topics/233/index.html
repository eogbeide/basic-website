    A. CURRENT BLUEPRINT VERSION + DIMENSION 1 COMPETENCY DOMAINS — WHY STUDENTS MISS IT The large biomedical overlap makes it tempting to postpone osteopathic-specific preparation until after Step 1-style content review. EASIEST CLUE If OPP/OMT, communication and systems scenarios are absent from your weekly plan, the blueprint is not being represented. 2. Blueprint percentages are minimum specifications, not a day-by-day study calendar DEEP EXPLANATION NBOME uses the blueprint to construct the exam; your study allocation must combine blueprint coverage with school requirements, personal performance data and recurring errors. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT A percentage looks like an easy scheduling formula, so learners may translate 12% into exactly 12% of study time. EASIEST CLUE Use the blueprint to guarantee coverage; use performance data to decide extra remediation time. 3. Non-knowledge competency domains are testable, not decorative DEEP EXPLANATION Patient care, practice-based learning, communication, professionalism and systems-based practice can appear inside clinical vignettes and require sequencing, judgment and osteopathic framing. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT They may feel less “content-heavy” than pathology or pharmacology, so learners defer them. EASIEST CLUE When two answers are medically plausible, ask which best reflects safe, patient-centered osteopathic practice and the task being tested.
