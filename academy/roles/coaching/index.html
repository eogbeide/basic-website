    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Run a measurable leadership-development engagement for one manager/leader and a small cohort: contract outcomes and confidentiality; conduct intake and stakeholder needs analysis; deliver a four-session coaching cycle using listening/GROW; give evidence-based feedback; facilitate one workshop; interpret a simulated or real assessment/360 data set; create a development plan; adapt coaching for an AI-enabled workflow/change; then evaluate reaction, learning, behavior and business/team results in a sponsor-ready impact report without breaching coaching confidentiality.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate coaching presence and question quality; clear outcome contracting; observable behavior change; safe feedback and boundaries; facilitation quality; appropriate use and limits of assessments; inclusive stakeholder judgment; learning-transfer evidence; measurable impact; and reflection on ethics, confidentiality, what changed and what did not.
