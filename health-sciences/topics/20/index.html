    CLINICAL SKILLS I + PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Begin behaving like a clinician from the first year: gather data accurately, examine the patient, communicate clearly and work safely within the healthcare system.

    Mastery checklist

    • Patient-centered interview, agenda setting and history of present illness.
    • Medication/allergy/social/family history and preventive care.
    • Vital signs and general survey.
    • Cardiac, pulmonary, abdominal, neurologic, musculoskeletal, skin and basic HEENT exams.
    • Oral case presentation and note structure.
    • Communication, informed consent, confidentiality, error disclosure and teamwork.
    • Basic patient safety, hand hygiene and infection prevention.

    You should be able to...

    • Obtain a focused and complete history.
    • Perform and verbalize the logic of a basic physical exam.
    • Present a patient in a concise problem-oriented format.
    • Write a structured note and problem list.
    • Recognize when supervision/escalation is required.
