    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Take one AI-native product opportunity from evidence to launch readiness: conduct user discovery and scope the workflow; choose model, retrieval, tool and agent architecture; prototype the human-AI experience; build an eval set from expected tasks and failures; define offline plus online quality, adoption, latency, reliability and cost metrics; run an experiment; specify feedback loops and human controls; assess safety, privacy and residual risk; then deliver a staged launch plan, roadmap, economics and executive decision memo.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate AI product judgment rather than feature enthusiasm: evidence-backed problem selection; technical fluency across models, RAG, tools and agents; measurable eval quality; strong human-AI interaction and failure recovery; explicit latency/cost/reliability tradeoffs; online experimentation and learning loops; governance and launch gates proportional to risk; credible business value and adoption metrics; and clear decisions about what not to automate, ship or scale.
