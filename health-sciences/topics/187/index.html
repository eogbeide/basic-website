Cumulative review is selective maintenance, not equal-time repetition of everything Deep explanationThe goal is to prevent important prior material from becoming inaccessible while the current block dominates attention. Sample older content by recency, recurrence, prior weakness and integration value rather than attempting a full Year 1 replay.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueLearners fear forgetting and may spread time so evenly that the current exam is underprepared. Maintain strong old material briefly; repair current or recurring weaknesses deeply.
    9. A readiness signal is stronger when multiple modalities agree Deep explanationA strong question-bank percentage can coexist with weak images, slow calculations or poor practical execution. Readiness is more credible when objective coverage, new-item performance, timing, representation-specific tasks and error stability point in the same direction.
