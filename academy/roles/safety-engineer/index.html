    Design a safety-assurance package for a real or simulated automated equipment/process launch: hazard register and task/JHA; standards/code matrix; preliminary risk assessment; machine/electrical and LOTO safeguards; PHA/MOC where relevant; FMEA/FTA or equivalent system-safety analysis; FAT/SAT and commissioning checks; incident/root-cause simulation; leading/lagging metrics; emergency controls; residual-risk acceptance; and an executive readiness memo.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove design-out-first judgment and traceable hazard-to-control logic; standards/code reasoning; machine, electrical and process safeguards; verification and commissioning evidence; MOC discipline; incident learning and corrective-action quality; quantified risk reduction; explicit residual risk; lifecycle governance; and concise communication to engineering, operations and leadership.
