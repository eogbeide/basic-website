    C. SIX-STEP AI VERIFICATION LOOP

    StepRequired action
    1. CommitAnswer or outline first when the task is meant to test you.
    2. GenerateUse AI for explanation, critique, contrast, question generation or organization.
    3. FlagMark claims that are current, numerical, safety-sensitive, policy-related or surprising.
    4. VerifyOpen the authoritative / primary / trusted source and inspect the exact claim.
    5. RetrieveClose both source and AI; reproduce the rule, mechanism or sequence from memory.
    6. TransferUse a new question, case, image, calculation or observed skill to prove the repair.
