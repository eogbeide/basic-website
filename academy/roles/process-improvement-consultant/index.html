    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Lead an end-to-end DMAIC improvement on a real or simulated operation. Define the customer/CTQ, charter and target; create SIPOC/value-stream/BPMN views and a trustworthy baseline; validate measurement quality; identify waste and variation; prove root causes with 5-Why/FMEA plus data; use SPC and process-mining evidence where available; design and pilot the future state; automate one repeatable step; quantify cost, quality, cycle-time, capacity and reliability impact; then deploy standard work, controls, owner/runbook documentation, KPI dashboard, operator engagement and a 30/60/90-day sustainment review.

    Proof Rubric

    Show a statistically and operationally credible baseline; distinguish common-cause from special-cause variation; validate root causes before prescribing countermeasures; prioritize failure risk explicitly; quantify before/after performance and confidence; demonstrate the automation under failure/exception cases; create usable standard work and control triggers; assign owners and escalation paths; and prove the gain is sustained through KPI/SPC evidence, Gemba/operator feedback and a documented continuous-improvement cadence.
