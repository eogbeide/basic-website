    ResourceDescriptionLink

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — E. ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Pathway-order gap - knew components but not where they belong or what enters/exits.
    • Regulation gap - memorized pathway but could not predict insulin/glucagon/allosteric direction.
    • Cofactor/vitamin gap - missed because the vitamin's biochemical role was disconnected from phenotype.
    • Inborn-error mechanism gap - recognized the disease but not substrate/product/lab consequences.
    • Metabolic-state gap - failed to integrate liver/muscle/adipose/RBC behavior across fed, fasting or alcohol states.
