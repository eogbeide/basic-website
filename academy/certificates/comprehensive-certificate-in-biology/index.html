Integrative Capstone

Design an integrated biological investigation around a gene, pathway, organism or ecological system. Build a mechanistic model from molecules to phenotype; formulate a falsifiable hypothesis; choose genetic, imaging, biochemical or ecological measurements; specify controls and sampling; analyze simulated or public data; assess alternative explanations; connect findings to evolution or physiology where relevant; and produce a reproducible research report plus a follow-up experiment plan.

Evidence Standard

  • Mechanistic coherence
  • genetic and molecular accuracy
  • appropriate level of biological organization
  • experimental controls
  • sampling and measurement quality
  • causal restraint
  • quantitative interpretation
  • uncertainty
  • reproducibility
  • integration across molecular, cellular, organismal and ecological scales. RELEASE QUALITY STANDARD: long-form academic instruction, explicit limitations and an inspectable capstone artifact - not just claimed
