Interview-Readiness Proof / Practice Loop

Complete an economist case: define a mechanism and hypotheses, specify an empirical design, interpret regression/experiment evidence, address identification threats, add strategic behavior and present a policy/business recommendation with limitations.

Pass Rubric

Correct economic mechanism; explicit assumptions; econometric validity; causal humility; strategic reasoning; uncertainty; scenario tradeoffs; decision relevance; clear explanation to non-specialists.
