Integrative Capstone

Day 1 - Pick an imaging or sensing use case; write measurable requirements for field, resolution, wavelength, F/#, detector and environment. Day 2 - Create the paraxial/ray model; choose lens architecture, stops and detector. Calculate diffraction limit and first-order image scale. Day 3 - Model aberrations and MTF/OTF; add tolerances, alignment sensitivities and a basic radiometry/SNR budget. Day 4 - Implement or reproduce the design in OpticStudio (or equivalent); test defocus, misalignment and one realistic manufacturing/thermal perturbation. Day 5 - Produce a verification matrix, plots/screenshots, risk register, failure analysis and redesign recommendation.

Evidence Standard

  • Evidence, not claims: linked calculations
  • ray/wave model
  • MTF or resolution evidence
  • detector/radiometry assumptions
  • tolerance/alignment sensitivity
  • simulation screenshots or plots
  • one failure/edge case
  • explicit tradeoffs
  • verification criteria
  • final design review
