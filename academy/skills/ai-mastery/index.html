7-Day AI Builder Bootcamp

Day 1 – Scope: pick a costly real workflow; define user, task, baseline, non-goals, risks and measurable success. Day 2 – Ground: build retrieval with source metadata and a small relevance test set; inspect failure cases. Day 3 – Act: add one tool/API/MCP integration with explicit permissions, timeout, validation and fallback. Day 4 – Evaluate: create representative cases, rubrics/graders and regression thresholds for quality and safety. Day 5 – Operate: instrument traces, latency, token/cost and tool failures; add retries only where evidence supports them. Day 6 – Red Team: test injection, unsafe tool requests, data leakage, ambiguity and over-authority; tighten boundaries. Day 7 – Ship: stage rollout, human escalation, rollback/runbook, executive demo and measured adoption/task success.

AI Mastery Capstone

Ship a production-style AI workflow with grounded context, at least one safe tool integration, an inspectable eval set, regression gates, traces, latency/cost metrics, explicit security/safety controls and a runbook. Defend when you would use prompt/context changes, RAG, fine-tuning, deterministic code or no AI at all.
