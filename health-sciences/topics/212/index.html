    THE 7-LINK CHAIN - 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION INTEGRATED CASES

    ShareXinf

    YEAR 2 | PAGE 51D - USE THE FULL CHAIN: NORMAL -> FAILURE -> MANIFESTATION -> DIFFERENTIAL -> CONFIRMATION -> TREATMENT ->

    COMPLICATIONS CASE RULE: DO NOT ANSWER FROM THE DISEASE NAME ALONE. FOR EACH STEM, STATE THE EARLIEST LINK YOU ARE SURE ABOUT, MOVE FORWARD CAUSALLY, AND IDENTIFY WHICH LINK THE QUESTION IS ACTUALLY TESTING. L. 10 SEVEN-LINK CASES

    #CASE7-LINK ANSWER LOGIC
    1Dyspnea, edema, low EF and rising creatinine after decompensated heart failure.Normal flow/filtration -> pump failure + venous congestion -> renal perfusion/pressure consequences. Separate disease progression, hemodynamic compensation and treatment effects before choosing the tested next step.
    2Sudden dyspnea after immobilization with hypoxemia and tachycardia.Normal V/Q -> perfusion interruption -> high-V/Q/dead-space physiology. Use risk + tempo + gas-exchange/hemodynamic clues to discriminate PE from pneumonia/asthma before confirmation.
    3Heavy proteinuria, edema and a new thrombotic event.Normal glomerular barrier -> protein loss -> low oncotic pressure + urinary loss of anticoagulant proteins. The thrombosis is a predictable downstream complication, not a separate disease list fact.
    4Hyperglycemia, ketones, high anion gap and volume depletion.Insulin deficiency -> lipolysis/ketogenesis + osmotic diuresis. Predict acid-base and total-body electrolyte deficits; distinguish immediate correction priorities from definitive diabetes management.
    5Microcytic anemia with low retic response and abnormal iron studies.Normal erythropoiesis -> substrate/production failure -> small RBCs with inadequate marrow response. Use iron-pattern discriminators before jumping to inherited microcytosis or marrow disease.
    6Fever, hypotension, thrombocytopenia and prolonged coagulation studies during severe infection.Infection/inflammation -> endothelial/coagulation activation -> consumption and organ dysfunction. Distinguish DIC-type physiology from isolated thrombocytopenia or inherited factor deficiency.
    7Hot swollen knee in an immunosuppressed patient with crystals found in fluid.Acute monoarthritis remains a differential problem: crystals explain inflammation but do not erase infection risk. Let host context and synovial microbiology determine whether the competing diagnosis remains active.
    8Photosensitive rash, arthritis, cytopenias and proteinuria.Multisystem immune-complex pattern -> organ manifestations. Serologies/complement confirm and stratify probability; renal involvement changes complication/severity thinking.
    9Aphasia and contralateral face/arm weakness with abrupt onset.Normal cortical language/motor mapping -> focal vascular failure -> dominant-hemisphere pattern. Localize + tempo before disease label, then choose the appropriate imaging/management sequence concept.
    10New rigidity, fever and autonomic instability after antipsychotic dose change.Normal dopamine pathways -> pharmacologic blockade/toxicity -> motor + autonomic manifestations. Medication history and tempo discriminate toxicity from psychiatric relapse or primary neurologic disease.
