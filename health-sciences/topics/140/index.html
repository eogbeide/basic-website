    N. CURRENT LICENSING-EXAM READINESS TOOLS

    LaneCurrent format / official signalHow Year 3 should feed it
    Step 2 CKexams on/after May 7, 2026: 16 x 30-minute blocks in one 9-hour session, up to items, no more than 20 items per block; minimum 55 minutes break + 5-minute 316 nal tutorial.Shelf questions are system blocks for Step 2. Preserve management, prevention, safety and cross-system integration rather than relearning each clerkship during dedicated.
    NBME CCSSACurrent Comprehensive Clinical Science Self-Assessment has 200 items: four 50-question sections. Standard-paced mode allows 75 minutes per section. It is designed to track Step 2 CK readiness.Use after enough core clerkships are complete to make a cumulative assessment meaningful; use longitudinal domain trends and question-level feedback.
    COMLEX Level 2-CECurrent 2026 format: 320 single-best-answer items, eight 40-question sections, two separately timed 4-hour sessions (8 hours total), with 60 minutes pooled break time. Competencies include OPP/OMM plus clinical care, communication, systems, professionalism and practice-based learning.Build the same shared clinical core as Step 2, then deliberately maintain OPP/OMM and osteopathic competency domains instead of creating a duplicate medical curriculum.
    COMSAE Phase 2Current Phase 2 contains 160 items in four 40-question sections; timed version is 4 hours and aligns to the Level 2-CE blueprint. NBOME cautions against using one COMSAE score as a stand-alone predictor.Use as one readiness signal alongside COMAT results, Qbank trends, curricular performance and your school's required benchmarks.

    O. LONGITUDINAL TRANSITION - FROM SHELF SILOS TO MIXED CLINICAL REASONING

    StageExecution
    Early core clerkshipsMost questions remain rotation-specific. Keep a very small cumulative retrieval lane so prior content does not disappear.
    Several core clerkships completedAdd one mixed clinical block weekly or every 1-2 weeks, depending on workload. Review whether old-clerkship misses are knowledge decay or new integration errors.
    End of core year / pre-dedicatedAudit unsampled systems, prevention, ethics/patient safety, biostatistics/EBM and cross-discipline management. Take an official cumulative readiness assessment early enough to remediate.
    DedicatedShift from specialty silos to mixed timed blocks. Use CCSSA for Step 2 or COMSAE Phase 2 / school-required tools for Level 2 as formal checkpoints. Keep targeted specialty review only for data-proven gaps.
    Final weekPractice current exam software, endurance, break strategy and short-block rhythm; retrieve high-yield algorithms and safety rules; protect sleep. Avoid large new resource additions.
