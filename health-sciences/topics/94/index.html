    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE

    ResourceDescriptionLink
    Days 1-2MSK mechanics + arthritis. Drill inflammatory vs mechanical, joint distribution, imaging and synovial-fluid cases.
    Days 3-4Autoimmune rheumatology. Build one phenotype -> antibody/complement -> organ complication -> therapy table; add vasculitis by vessel size.
    Day 5Bone, muscle and tumors. Practice age/location/radiograph tumor cases plus osteoporosis/osteomyelitis and weakness localization.
    Day 6Derm morphology + inflammatory/infectious/malignant lesions. Complete a timed mixed block using images whenever available.
    Day 7Re-test every miss on NEW vignettes/images; redraw only the unstable joint-pattern, antibody, vasculitis and lesion-morphology maps.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — SCHOOL-EXAM MASTERY GATE

    Proceed when you can classify joint pain before naming disease, interpret synovial fluid/antibodies/imaging in context, recognize high-yield bone and rheumatic patterns, describe a skin lesion precisely, and identify the dangerous MSK/derm presentations that require urgent action.
