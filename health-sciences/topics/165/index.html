    The original Review Expansion established the Academy's core review philosophy: every phase needs block/course review -> cumulative review -> professional-exam review; review should be blueprint-driven; questions should follow content review; explanations should become the remediation map;

    and readiness should use multiple signals rather than resource completion. This expansion turns that philosophy into an operating system.

    A. THREE REVIEW JOBS - DO NOT ASK ONE SESSION TO DO ALL THREE

    Review jobQuestion it answersTypical inputsDone when...
    Block / course reviewCan I perform on the school's current objectives and modalities?School objectives, lectures/labs, practical images, local cases, targeted questions.Every objective is retrievable; weak modalities are repaired; one unfamiliar mixed check is passed.
    Cumulative spiralCan I still retrieve prior material after new material has displaced it?Older blocks, spaced error rules, mixed questions, images/graphs from prior systems.Old content survives weekly sampling without broad relearning.
    Professional-exam reviewCan I reorganize school knowledge around the national blueprint and standardized vignette decisions?Official blueprint, primary Qbank, mixed timed work, official readiness tools.Coverage is complete; weak domains are repaired; transfer and endurance are stable.
