    YEAR 2 SYSTEM BLOCKS

    ENDOCRINE / METABOLISM — Core checklist

    • Hypothalamic-pituitary axes and feedback.
    • Thyroid, adrenal, diabetes, calcium/parathyroid and bone disease.
    • Endocrine tumors and endocrine emergencies.
    • Insulin, oral diabetes drugs, thyroid, steroid and bone pharmacology.

    ENDOCRINE / METABOLISM — Mastery outcomes

    • Interpret hormone patterns.
    • Recognize endocrine emergencies.
    • Choose mechanism-based therapy and monitoring. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    REPRODUCTIVE + PREGNANCY FOUNDATIONS — Core checklist

    • Male/female reproductive physiology and pathology.
    • Menstrual cycle, fertility and contraception.
    • Pregnancy physiology, early/late pregnancy complications and fetal development.
    • Breast pathology and reproductive cancers.

    REPRODUCTIVE + PREGNANCY FOUNDATIONS — Mastery outcomes

    • Reason through abnormal bleeding/infertility.
    • Recognize common pregnancy pathophysiology.
    • Integrate embryology with congenital/reproductive disease. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    SYSTEM BLOCK ASSESSMENT

    Complete a targeted question set after each major topic, then a timed mixed block at the end of the system. Remediate by mechanism, not by memorizing the question.
