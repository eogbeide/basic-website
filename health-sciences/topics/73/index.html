    LOCALIZE THE SYNDROME BEFORE NAMING THE DISEASE

    Use SITE -> PATHOLOGIC PROCESS -> SYMPTOM / LAB PATTERN -> TEST -> TREATMENT. Separate luminal/mucosal disease from pancreas/biliary disease, hepatocellular injury from cholestasis, and upper from lower GI bleeding.

    B. CLINICAL GI / LIVER VIDEOS - CORE + INTEGRATION — DISEASE-INTEGRATION CHECK

    • Dysphagia/abdominal pain/diarrhea/bleeding: localize by symptom timing, stool pattern, hemodynamics and red flags before selecting a test.
    • Liver: distinguish hepatocellular injury, cholestasis and synthetic dysfunction; connect bilirubin pattern to prehepatic/hepatic/posthepatic mechanisms.
    • Pancreas/biliary: use pain pattern, enzymes and imaging to separate pancreatitis, biliary obstruction and related complications.
