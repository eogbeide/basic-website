    Day 7Error-driven re-test with NEW problems; update one-page formula/interpretation sheet only with recurring misses.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — E. BIOSTATS / EBM ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY

    • Design-selection gap - confused how subjects were sampled or what the study can estimate.
    • Denominator gap - knew the formula name but used the wrong population in the denominator.
    • Direction/interpretation gap - calculated correctly but reversed exposure/outcome or misread what the statistic means.
    • Validity gap - identified an association but missed bias, confounding, attrition or lack of generalizability.
    • Inference gap - treated p-value, confidence interval, power, significance and clinical importance as interchangeable.

    D. 7-DAY SCHOOL-EXAM REVIEW CYCLE — SCHOOL-EXAM GATE

    Proceed when you can solve unseen calculation and interpretation problems without a formula prompt, identify the dominant validity threat, and explain every result in plain clinical language.
