    YEAR 2 | PAGE 22C - ECG, MURMURS, HEMODYNAMICS, DIAGNOSTICS + PHARM

    C. WHAT SCHOOL EXAMS COMMONLY REQUIRE — Hemodynamics / PV loops

    Translate preload, afterload, contractility, compliance and valve disease into loop/Wiggers changes, CO, EF and compensatory physiology.

    C. WHAT SCHOOL EXAMS COMMONLY REQUIRE — ECG / conduction

    Rate, rhythm, axis, intervals, AV/bundle blocks, ischemic ST-T changes, electrolyte effects and common tachy/bradyarrhythmias.

    Murmurs / valves

    Timing, location, radiation, intensity/maneuver changes, chamber consequences and echo correlation.

    Ischemia / MI

    Coronary territory, supply-demand logic, biomarkers, time-course pathology, mechanical/electrical complications and initial diagnostic sequence.

    HF / cardiomyopathy

    Systolic vs diastolic physiology, congestion, neurohormonal compensation, hemodynamics, imaging and mechanism-based therapy.

    Congenital / vascular

    Left-to-right vs right-to-left shunts, Eisenmenger logic, coarctation, aneurysm/dissection and hypertension consequences.

    Cardio pharmacology

    Antianginal, antihypertensive, HF, antiarrhythmic, antiplatelet/anticoagulant mechanisms, signature toxicities and physiologic effects.

    D. 7-DAY CARDIOVASCULAR REVIEW CYCLE — Days 1-2

    Rebuild cardiac cycle, PV loops, CO/BP relationships, conduction and ECG basics without notes; then do targeted physiology questions.

    D. 7-DAY CARDIOVASCULAR REVIEW CYCLE — Days 3-4

    CAD/MI + HF/cardiomyopathy + valves. For each case predict the hemodynamics before reading the options.

    Day 5

    Arrhythmias, hypertension/vascular and congenital lesions; integrate drug mechanisms and adverse-effect patterns.

    Day 6

    Timed mixed block with ECGs, murmurs, graphs/PV loops, imaging and pharmacology; classify every miss by reasoning failure.

    Day 7

    Targeted repair only; re-test each weak mechanism on NEW tracings/graphs/vignettes and finish with a cumulative mixed set.

    E. CARDIOVASCULAR ERROR-LOG TAXONOMY — TAG THE REASON FOR THE MISS

    HEMODYNAMIC DIRECTION - wrong preload/afterload/contractility/PV prediction. ECG LOCALIZATION - lead, territory or conduction logic failed. MURMUR MECHANISM - timing/maneuver/chamber consequence was not derived. THERAPY MECHANISM - target, physiologic effect or signature toxicity was unclear.
