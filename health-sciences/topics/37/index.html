    Longitudinal Step 2 strategy

    • Do not postpone Step 2 learning until dedicated: each clerkship is a Step 2 system block.
    • Finish most shelf-aligned questions during the rotation; review incorrects and build a concise error log.
    • Use NBME CSMS/COMAT-SE for rotation readiness; use NBME CCSSA for cumulative Step 2 readiness.
    • During dedicated, switch from specialty silos to mixed timed blocks and management-heavy reasoning.
    • Practice health maintenance, patient safety, ethics, communication and epidemiology deliberately; these are explicit exam domains.
