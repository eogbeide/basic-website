    H. NEUROLOGY QUESTION-REVIEW ALGORITHM 1. Name the syndrome: focal deficit, encephalopathy, seizure, headache, movement disorder, weakness, sensory loss or dizziness/ataxia? 2. Use tempo: seconds-minutes suggests vascular/seizure; hours-days may be infectious/inflammatory/toxic; relapsing/progressive changes the list. 3. Localize before diagnosis: cortex, brainstem, cerebellum, cord, root/plexus, peripheral nerve, NMJ or muscle? 4. Identify the dangerous alternative: stroke/hemorrhage, meningitis/encephalitis, status, cord compression, herniation or respiratory neuromuscular failure? 5. Choose the next decisive action: stabilize, CT/CTA, MRI, LP, EEG, electrodiagnostics, targeted labs or treatment now? 6. Review the best distractor: what single clue localizes it elsewhere or makes its timing/management sequence wrong? I. 7-DAY NEUROLOGY EXAM FINISH
