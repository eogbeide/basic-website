    SHELF / COMAT REVIEW - PSYCHIATRY + NEUROLOGY + FAMILY MEDICINE

    ShareXinf

    Psychiatry

    • MSE, suicide/violence risk, mood, psychosis, anxiety/trauma, substance use, personality, child psychiatry, sleep, medication adverse effects.
    • Differentiate look-alike diagnoses by duration, functional impairment and exclusion criteria; know emergency safety and medication toxicity.

    Neurology

    • Localization before disease label; stroke, seizure, headache, movement, demyelination, neuromuscular, peripheral nerve and neuro emergencies.
    • Strong Medicine ECG is not neuro; use bedside neuro exam via Stanford Medicine 25 and supervised examination.

    Family Medicine

    • Preventive care, screening, vaccination, chronic disease, common ambulatory complaints, dermatology/MSK, women’s health, pediatrics and behavioral health.
