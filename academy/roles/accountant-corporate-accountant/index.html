    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Close a simulated month end to end: post journal entries and accruals; reconcile cash and key balance-sheet accounts; apply ASC 606 to a customer contract and inventory costing to a transaction set; prepare P&L, balance sheet and cash-flow statements; investigate fluxes; document COSO/SOX key controls; produce an audit PBC package and accounting memo; configure an ERP/close workflow; automate one reconciliation with exceptions and human approval.

    Proof Rubric

    Produce balanced and supportable books; tie journal entries and reconciliations to evidence; apply GAAP consistently; explain revenue/inventory judgments; complete close on schedule; show ERP/reporting integrity; document control design and audit evidence; write a defensible accounting conclusion; demonstrate automation with exception handling, approvals and audit trail.
