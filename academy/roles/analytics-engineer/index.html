    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Build and operate a production analytics product for a GTM, product or operations domain. Start from stakeholder decisions and source/event contracts; design facts and dimensions at explicit grain; implement SQL/dbt-style transformations in Git; define canonical metrics and a semantic layer; add unit, data and freshness tests plus CI; publish a governed self-service dashboard and decision memo; instrument lineage, ownership and data-quality alerts; expose one AI/conversational analytics experience constrained to trusted semantics plus one lightweight custom data app; document cost/performance and change-management tradeoffs; then use adoption and issue telemetry to prioritize the next data-product iteration.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate reproducibility and product judgment: correct grain and model design; canonical metric definitions; tested/versioned transformations; lineage and freshness evidence; self-service without metric drift; a persuasive decision narrative; clear ownership/SLOs; governed AI queries grounded in trusted semantics; maintainable custom-app code; and a traceable link from business question to data contract to product outcome.
