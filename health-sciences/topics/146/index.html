    The strongest transition is not “finish clerkships, then start Step 2.” Each clerkship is a system block. Dedicated should compress and integrate knowledge already built through patient care, shelf/COMAT questions and error-driven remediation. C. THREE-PHASE STUDY ARCHITECTURE

    PhaseExecutionExit condition
    During clerkshipsFinish most shelf-aligned UWorld/AMBOSS questions; convert patients into illness scripts; complete NBME CSMS or COMAT-SE where applicable; keep a one-page error taxonomy.Can solve specialty questions by next-step logic, not just recognize diagnoses.
    Transition / baselineSwitch to mixed questions; take a cumulative readiness assessment; rank weaknesses by frequency x severity x reversibility.You know the 3-5 domains actually limiting performance.
    DedicatedMixed timed blocks + targeted repair + periodic official assessment. Increase endurance while reducing repeat errors. Keep OPP/OMM active for Level 2.Performance is stable across systems and management sequences under time pressure.

    D. DAILY DEDICATED LOOP

    • Timed mixed questions: simulate the decision environment. Mark guessed and low-confidence corrects, not only incorrects.
    • Deep review: for each miss identify whether the failure was recognition, discriminator, next-test sequence, treatment sequence, medication safety, prevention/disposition, ethics/safety or timing.
    • Targeted repair: one focused source for the precise gap; do not restart an entire subject because of one missed mechanism.
    • Retrieval: close the notes and reproduce the rule/algorithm from memory. Then solve a new question testing the same decision.
    • Spiral: every day include material from prior rotations so “fixed” weaknesses stay fixed.
