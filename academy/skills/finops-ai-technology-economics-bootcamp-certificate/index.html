Bootcamp Mastery Certificate B46

Manage technology spend as an engineering system: allocation, unit economics, FOCUS, forecasting, AI/LLM spend and cost-quality-latency tradeoffs tied to business value.

Build-Along Checkpoints

  • Checkpoint 1: Ingest a realistic multi-account/cloud cost dataset; normalize dimensions, define ownership and create a reconciled baseline that ties engineering resources to spend.
  • Checkpoint 2: Implement allocation rules for tags, shared services and unallocated spend; reconcile totals, document rule ownership and produce showback/chargeback-ready views.
  • Checkpoint 3: Define at least two unit-economic metrics such as cost per customer, request, feature or transaction; build a dashboard that connects spend to usage and business output.
  • Checkpoint 4: Model optimization levers for commitments, rightsizing, storage/data and workload scheduling; distinguish realized savings from theoretical recommendations.
  • Checkpoint 5: Build an AI economics model covering tokens or GPU/inference consumption, model choice/routing, caching and quality/latency tradeoffs; compute cost per successful task.
  • Checkpoint 6: Forecast spend and variance, add anomaly detection and governance thresholds; inject a cost spike, investigate root cause and produce an engineering/finance decision memo.

7-Day Independent Capstone

  • Day 1: Define technology scopes, stakeholders and decision cadence; create a cost taxonomy covering cloud, data/platform and AI spend with explicit ownership and value questions.
  • Day 2: Normalize the billing dataset, map accounts/projects/products and allocate shared costs; prove monthly reconciliation and quantify the remaining unallocated percentage.
  • Day 3: Build unit-cost metrics and a decision dashboard; connect infrastructure/AI consumption to product usage, business volume and at least one outcome metric.
  • Day 4: Create optimization scenarios for rates and workloads; model savings, implementation effort, risk and forecast impact and separate recommendations from realized outcomes.
  • Day 5: Build an AI/token economics simulator comparing at least two model or serving configurations on cost, task quality, latency and capacity; identify the efficient frontier.
  • Day 6: Add forecast/budget variance, anomaly controls and an incident workflow; simulate runaway spend or demand change and document containment plus stakeholder communication.
  • Day 7: Publish cost model, normalized dataset sample, allocation logic, unit-economics dashboard, AI economics analysis, forecast, incident evidence and executive decision memo.

FinOps - Allocate, Forecast & Optimize AI Economics

Build a decision-ready technology economics system rather than a savings spreadsheet: normalize cost data, allocate spend, calculate unit economics, model AI token/GPU costs, forecast variance and prove optimization outcomes.

Proof Rubric — What Must Be Shown

  • COST DATA: normalized + reconciled source
  • ALLOCATION: shared cost + ownership rules
  • UNIT ECONOMICS: cost linked to demand/value
  • FORECASTING: variance + scenario evidence
  • OPTIMIZATION: realized vs theoretical savings
  • AI ECONOMICS: token/GPU + model tradeoffs
  • VALUE: quality / latency / cost decisions
  • GOVERNANCE: thresholds, incident + accountability
