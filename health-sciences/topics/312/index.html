    CONTROL-CENTER RULE: THE DASHBOARD EXISTS TO MAKE ONE DECISION - WHAT SHOULD I KEEP, REPAIR, RE-TEST OR DROP NEXT? DO NOT TURN IT INTO ANOTHER NOTE-TAKING PROJECT. USE THE FEWEST METRICS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGE ACTION.

    F. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION DASHBOARD DECISIONS

    #PatternBest next decision
    1Lecture/video completion is high, but closed-book recall is poor.Reduce passive completion; use retrieval before reopening content and track what survives without cues.
    2Qbank percentage rises, but guessed/slow corrects remain high.Review fragile corrects and reasoning reproducibility; do not let headline accuracy hide instability.
    3One rare miss is dramatic; three ordinary errors recur weekly.Prioritize the recurring cluster; rare isolated detail remains lower priority unless high-consequence.
    4Targeted cardiology sets improve; mixed blocks do not.Return to mixed discrimination; remaining gap is transfer under uncertainty, not cardiology exposure.
    5Text questions are strong; histology/ECG/graphs repeatedly fail.Repair the same knowledge through the weak representation and retest on unseen images/data.
    6A repaired rule is correct immediately but wrong again five days later.Treat repair as failed retention; change encoding/retrieval method and schedule another delayed retest.
    7Late-block accuracy falls despite good early performance.Train endurance/pacing/break strategy; reduce low-yield hours that worsen fatigue.
    8Three resources are open and the dashboard is not improving.Choose one primary tool by job; pause duplicates; keep only targeted supplements with a stop rule.
    9Official/school assessment is lower than daily Qbank performance.Investigate repeats, selection bias, conditions, timing and domain mix before adding a new curriculum.
    10Recent signals agree and the weak list is small.Consolidate, maintain, taper broad new learning and protect sleep/execution rather than expanding the stack.

    H. FINAL PERSONALIZED-MASTERY GATE

    GatePass condition

    RetrievalCore mechanisms, rules and algorithms can be reproduced without notes or AI.
    TransferFresh mixed questions/images/cases are stable; strongest distractors can be rejected for the right reason.
    Error stabilityRepeat clusters are shrinking; repaired errors stay repaired after delay.
    Timing / enduranceRepresentative timed work finishes without meaningful late-session collapse.
    Representation / skillsRelevant images/data/procedures/communication transfer beyond text-only recognition.
    Recovery / sustainabilityThe plan preserves next-day retrieval, attention and required school/clinical duties.
    GovernanceEvery active resource/metric has a job; weekly evidence controls keep/repair/retest/drop decisions.
