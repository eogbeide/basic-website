    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Run an operations-transformation engagement for a real or simulated client. Diagnose the current state through stakeholder interviews, data and process observation; build a value-stream/BPMN map and baseline KPIs; benchmark performance; design the future operating model with roles, governance, controls and human/AI-agent handoffs; use process-mining or operational data to validate bottlenecks; prototype one automation/agent workflow; build a quantified business case and benefits register; then deliver a phased transformation roadmap, change-impact/adoption plan, KPI review cadence and executive steering deck.

    Proof Rubric

    Show a traceable current-state fact base; distinguish symptoms from root causes; quantify cost, cycle time, quality, CX, risk and capacity where relevant; defend operating-model and automation tradeoffs; specify safe agent autonomy and human escalation; connect recommendations to business value and measurable benefits; sequence implementation with owners, risks and dependencies; and communicate a concise executive narrative that can survive challenge from operations, finance and technology leaders.
