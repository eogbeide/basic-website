    PHARMACOLOGY FOUNDATIONS

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Build a durable framework for drug action, dosing logic, interactions and toxicity before memorizing system-specific agents.

    Mastery checklist

    • Pharmacokinetics: absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination, clearance, half-life, steady state.
    • Pharmacodynamics: receptors, agonists/antagonists, potency, efficacy, dose-response and therapeutic index.
    • Drug metabolism and CYP interactions.
    • Adverse drug reactions, contraindications and therapeutic monitoring.
    • Autonomic pharmacology foundations.
    • Principles of safe prescribing and medication reconciliation.

    You should be able to...

    • Interpret dose-response curves and PK relationships.
    • Predict interaction and toxicity patterns.
    • Distinguish potency from efficacy and competitive from noncompetitive effects.
    • Explain why renal/hepatic dysfunction changes dosing.
    • Use mechanisms to learn later drug classes efficiently.
