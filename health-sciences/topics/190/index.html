    5. Integration means one mechanism can be expressed in several disciplines Deep explanationA single case may contain anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology and microbiology. Integration is the ability to move between those representations without treating them as separate facts.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueCourses are taught in modules, so learners store the same disease mechanism in separate folders. Use the chain cause -> mechanism -> finding -> test -> treatment across disciplines.
    6. A compressed map is valuable only if it can regenerate detail Deep explanationOne-page maps should act as retrieval cues for a richer mental model. If the learner can recite the sheet but cannot solve a new vignette, the compression has become another memorized artifact.
    Why learners miss it Easiest clueSummary sheets feel efficient and familiar, so they can hide shallow recall. After every map, solve one new case without looking at it.
