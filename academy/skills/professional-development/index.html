Communication, influence, leadership, strategy, execution, business fluency, career capital, AI-era work

Built for career-long transfer rather than a single job. The pathway combines executive communication, stakeholder influence, leadership, operating judgment, business fluency and visible proof of impact. Learners should adapt depth to level: individual contributor → manager → senior manager → director → executive.

Benchmark: Stanford GSB + TED + Chicago Booth leadership/negotiation curricula (current Sep 2026)

Areas of Development

1. Communication + Executive Presence

Clear writing, spontaneous speaking, presentations, Q&A, listening and decision-ready synthesis.

2. Storytelling + Data

Turn analysis into a narrative with evidence, implications, options and an explicit decision ask.

3. Influence + Negotiation

Stakeholder mapping, interests, tradeoffs, conflict, persuasion, boundary setting and durable agreements.

4. Leadership + Feedback

Direction, coaching, delegation, accountability, candor, recognition and difficult conversations.

5. Team Effectiveness

Psychological safety, decision rights, operating norms, inclusion, conflict repair and high-quality meetings.

6. Strategy + Decision Making

Diagnosis, choices, uncertainty, resource allocation, scenarios, pre-mortems and stop-doing decisions.

7. Execution + Operating Rhythm

Goals, roadmap/program plan, dependencies, risks, KPI reviews, escalation and benefits realization.

8. Career Capital + Network

Skill portfolio, sponsorship, reputation, networking, mentoring, interviewing and visible artifacts of impact.

6-Week Professional Development Sprint

Week 1 – Communicate: write a one-page executive memo and deliver a 5-minute briefing; record it and revise for clarity. Week 2 – Influence: build a stakeholder map and run a negotiation simulation; document interests, BATNA/tradeoffs and outcome. Week 3 – Lead: practice one coaching/feedback conversation and one delegation contract with observable expectations. Week 4 – Strategize: write a decision memo with diagnosis, options, economics, risks, recommendation and a stop-doing choice. Week 5 – Operate: create a KPI tree, review cadence, risk/dependency log and escalation mechanism for a real initiative. Week 6 – AI + Career Capital: automate or augment one workflow with AI and update your visible portfolio of impact.
