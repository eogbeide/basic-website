    LANEMUST RETRIEVE WITHOUT NOTESHIGH-DISCRIMINATION TRANSFER
    Pain + mechanicsJoint vs periarticular vs bone vs muscle; traumatic/overuse vs inflammatory pattern; ROM, strength, gait and biomechanics.Use stiffness duration, activity effect, warmth/swelling, distribution and true weakness before choosing a disease label.
    Acute monoarthritisSeptic arthritis vs gout/pseudogout vs trauma; synovial WBC, Gram/culture and crystal shape/birefringence.When infection is plausible, joint aspiration is the discriminating lane; serum uric acid does not replace synovial analysis.
    Bone + muscleFracture/healing, osteoporosis/osteomalacia, osteomyelitis, tumors; myopathy vs neuropathy/NMJ vs pain-limited motion.For bone lesions use age + location + margin/matrix; for weakness use reflexes, sensation, distribution and CK context.
    RA / CTD / SpARA, SLE, Sjogren, systemic sclerosis, inflammatory myopathy, antiphospholipid; axial/psoriatic/reactive/IBD-associated disease.Start from phenotype and organ pattern; let antibodies/complement shift probability rather than define the diagnosis alone.
    Vasculitis + therapyLarge/medium/small vessel patterns; ischemic territory, renal/lung/skin/nerve clues; steroids, DMARD/biologic mechanisms and toxicities.Classify vessel size and threatened organ before overinterpreting ANCA or choosing therapy.

    B. TOP 10 MOST-MISSED SYSTEM CONCEPTS - #1 TO #3

    # / CONCEPTDEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    1. Joint pattern before disease nameMechanical pain often worsens with use and has shorter stiffness; inflammatory disease more often has prolonged morning stiffness, warmth/swelling, systemic features and characteristic joint distribution. Crystal/infectious disease is often acute and intensely inflammatory.Students memorize disease lists and jump directly from one clue to a label.Ask mechanical, inflammatory, crystal/infectious, or neoplastic? before naming the condition.
    2. Acute monoarthritis is a fluid problem until proved otherwiseWhen septic arthritis is plausible, synovial analysis is central because crystals, Gram/culture and cell count discriminate conditions that can look nearly identical clinically. A crystal does not automatically exclude infection.Learners overvalue serum uric acid or a familiar gout history and underweight the danger of infection.Hot swollen single joint + systemic risk = think aspiration and infection exclusion.
    3. Autoantibodies are probability-shifting evidenceAnti-CCP, dsDNA/Sm, complement, ANCA and other serologies are most useful after the phenotype, organ pattern and pretest probability are established. Positive tests can occur without the target disease.Memorizing antibody-disease pairs makes a positive laboratory result feel definitive.Build phenotype -> organ pattern -> serology, not serology -> diagnosis.
