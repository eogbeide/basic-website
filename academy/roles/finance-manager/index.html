    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own the finance operating rhythm for a real or simulated business unit: build a driver-based annual plan and rolling forecast across revenue, cost, headcount and capex; connect P&L, cash flow and operating drivers; run monthly variance and real-time scenario analyses; evaluate at least two capital-allocation choices with ROI/NPV/ROIC and downside sensitivities; automate a recurring reporting workflow; create a KPI decision dashboard and executive narrative; then use AI on one finance workflow with source checks, approval boundaries, audit trail and model-risk controls.

    Proof Rubric

    Demonstrate model integrity and traceable assumptions; reconcile plan, forecast and actuals; explain material P&L and cash-flow drivers; quantify forecast error and scenario sensitivity; defend capital-allocation choices; translate metrics into decision-ready narrative; show automation/data-quality and controllership controls; document AI inputs, outputs, human review and residual risk; and quantify financial or business impact.
