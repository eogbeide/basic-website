    F. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION AI-USE DECISIONS

    #SituationBest decision
    1Unsure about current Step/COMLEX formatOpen the official exam source first; use AI only to explain or organize what the source says.
    2You cannot explain acid-base physiologyAttempt a blank-page model, then use AI for a targeted mechanism repair; retest with new problems.
    3You know the topic but keep choosing the same distractorAsk AI to contrast the two options and critique your discriminator; verify the rule and solve fresh items.
    4AI generated 20 questions and one answer seems oddStop the set, verify the disputed key/rationale, then repair or discard flawed items before further use.
    5A generated citation supports a surprising claimOpen the source; confirm it exists and actually supports the statement before using it.
    6A course assignment does not clearly state whether AI is allowedCheck the syllabus/faculty policy before using AI on the graded task; do not infer permission.
    7You want to paste a real patient note for feedbackDo not use an unapproved consumer tool. Use a synthetic case or an institution-approved workflow.
    8AI gives a polished oral-presentation scriptRe-present from memory in your own words and get human feedback; do not equate generated fluency with competence.
    9Your resource stack is already overloadedDo not add AI as another full curriculum. Give it one distinct job with an output and stop rule.
    10AI makes studying faster but unaided performance is flatReduce dependence; shift time to retrieval, questions, observed skills and delayed transfer.

    G. FINAL AI-LEARNING MASTERY GATE

    GatePass condition
    AuthorityCurrent school / official / guideline source used for policy, exam-definition and other time-sensitive high-stakes claims.
    Independent reasoningLearner commits before rescue and can reproduce the core reasoning without AI.
    VerificationImportant claims and citations are opened and checked at the appropriate source level.
    PrivacyNo PHI or confidential data enter an unapproved tool; data are minimized.
    Academic integrityUse matches the exact course/assessment policy, including disclosure or prohibition.
    TransferRepair is confirmed with a new question/case/image/calculation or supervised skill.
    Tool governanceAI has one named job, measurable output and stop rule - not an unlimited second curriculum.
