    YEAR 2 SYSTEM BLOCKS

    RENAL / URINARY — Core checklist

    • GFR, autoregulation, tubular handling, osmolality and acid-base.
    • AKI/CKD, glomerular disease, nephritic/nephrotic syndromes, stones and urinary tract disease.
    • Electrolyte disorders and volume states.
    • Diuretics, RAAS agents and renal dosing principles.

    RENAL / URINARY — Mastery outcomes

    • Localize renal pathology from labs/urinalysis.
    • Solve acid-base/electrolyte problems.
    • Predict diuretic effects and complications. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    GI / LIVER / NUTRITION — Core checklist

    • GI motility, digestion/absorption and hepatic physiology.
    • Esophageal, gastric, intestinal, pancreatic, biliary and liver disease.
    • Malabsorption, inflammatory bowel disease, GI bleeding and GI cancers.
    • Nutrition, vitamins and GI pharmacology.

    GI / LIVER / NUTRITION — Mastery outcomes

    • Localize GI disease by symptom pattern.
    • Interpret liver tests and common GI workups.
    • Integrate nutrition and medication mechanisms. Use Pathoma for pathology, UWorld Step 1 or AMBOSS for vignette practice, and your school lectures for local objectives.

    SYSTEM BLOCK ASSESSMENT

    Complete a targeted question set after each major topic, then a timed mixed block at the end of the system. Remediate by mechanism, not by memorizing the question.
