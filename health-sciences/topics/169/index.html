    What receives priority?Local objectives, faculty emphasis, required practical/image content, current skills expectations and safety behaviors.Current school requirements can outrank national-board frequency for the present block; maintenance priority can change later.
    What does not count as completion?Watching every lecture twice, rereading notes, highlighting, finishing a playlist, or recognizing an answer explanation.Completion is not evidence unless the learner can retrieve and transfer under the expected conditions.
