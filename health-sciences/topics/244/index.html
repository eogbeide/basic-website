    J. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION DEDICATED / READINESS DECISIONS — K. TEST-DAY EXECUTION CHECK

    • Know the current structure: 8 sections x 40 questions; two 4-hour testing sessions; section review is available only before advancing to the next section.
    • Plan the 60-minute pooled break before test day; use scheduled breaks deliberately and follow the current NBOME administration rules.
    • During the tutorial, confirm headphones/audio and interface preferences; audiovisual elements may appear.
    • For difficult items: compress the presentation, identify the clinical task/competency, eliminate unsafe or nonresponsive options, choose, flag selectively and protect section timing.
    • Use the final hours before the exam for logistics, sleep and calm retrieval - not broad new learning.
