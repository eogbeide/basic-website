    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own one growth product area end to end. Define the growth equation and North Star input tree; instrument acquisition -> activation -> retention -> expansion -> monetization with an event taxonomy and data-quality checks; interview and observe users to identify time-to-value friction; segment cohorts and identify activation behaviors; build a prioritized experiment portfolio with hypotheses, power/sample-size assumptions, guardrails and rollout rules; run or simulate at least three experiments; model LTV/CAC/payback and pricing tradeoffs; ship one winning change; then propose a 90-day lifecycle/AI-personalization roadmap with privacy, trust and stop/scale criteria.

    Proof Rubric

    Prove durable growth, not vanity metrics: clear growth model and activation definition; trustworthy instrumentation; cohort/funnel evidence; customer insight linked to hypotheses; statistically defensible tests and guardrails; retention and expansion effects; monetization/LTV economics; cross-functional execution; user-trust/privacy checks; explicit scale/rollback criteria; and evidence that experiment results changed the roadmap.
