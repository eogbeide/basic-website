Integrative Capstone

Launch and operate a venture through an evidence-based founder cycle. Interview at least 15 target customers; define a beachhead segment and value proposition; prototype an MVP and run usability/demand tests; win or simulate a credible path to the first five customers; build pricing, revenue model, unit economics and 18-month cash/runway scenarios; create a founder-led GTM and growth experiment backlog; form the company structure and key founder/partner terms; prepare a financing strategy and investor data room; build hiring/culture/decision mechanisms for the next stage; identify one AI-native product or operating advantage and test its economics, reliability and trust risks; finish with a board/investor update showing traction, learning, risks and the next scale/pivot/stop decision.

Evidence Standard

Every Entrepreneurship bridge is a full workshop, lecture or complete founder/operator talk from Harvard, MIT, Stanford or Y Combinator. The set intentionally favors 45-90+ minute resources and excludes curriculum pages, registration-only pages and low-value short explainers.
