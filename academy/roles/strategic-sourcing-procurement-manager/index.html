    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Own a strategic sourcing category for a real or simulated AI/data-center/enterprise-spend portfolio. Build the spend baseline, demand forecast and supplier-market map; create category strategy and sourcing waves; issue an RFP/RFQ with weighted evaluation criteria; build TCO/should-cost and scenario models; negotiate pricing, capacity, SLA/quality, risk and change-order terms; create supplier scorecards and QBR governance; map continuity and concentration risk with mitigation/dual-source options; automate one procurement workflow with approval/audit controls; then deliver an award memo and executive sourcing review with savings, value, capacity and risk outcomes.

    Proof Rubric

    Show procurement value beyond unit-price savings: defensible category and supplier evidence; auditable TCO/should-cost assumptions; fair and traceable supplier selection; negotiation and contract discipline; supplier scorecards and corrective-action logic; capacity/NPI and resilience planning; finance/engineering/legal alignment; scenario and downside analysis; automation with approval, provenance and exception handling; measurable savings/value, lead-time, quality, capacity and risk impact; explain when partnership, dual sourcing, redesign or no-award is the better decision.
