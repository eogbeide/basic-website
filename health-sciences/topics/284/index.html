    10. FINAL READINESS SHOULD BECOME SIMPLER, MORE STABLE AND MORE REPRODUCIBLE

    DEEP EXPLANATIONWHY STUDENTS MISS ITEASIEST CLUE
    Near the exam, the plan should narrow: fewer recurring errors, fewer unresolved broad domains, stable timing, familiar interfaces and increasingly concordant recent evidence. Last-minute resource expansion is usually a warning sign, not a readiness signal.Anxiety creates pressure to add new resources or chase isolated weaknesses even when the evidence is already stable.Ask: Are my remaining problems fewer, narrower and predictable? Stable narrowing is more reassuring than frantic breadth.
