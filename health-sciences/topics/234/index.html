EASIEST CLUE After answering, label both the clinical presentation and the competency/task being tested. 5. Wellness/community health deserves longitudinal attention DEEP EXPLANATION Community Health and Wellness carries a 12% minimum and can include prevention, population context, risk reduction, screening, counseling and systems-based reasoning. WHY STUDENTS MISS IT It is easy to postpone because it does not feel like a classic organ-system block. EASIEST CLUE If your mixed sets contain almost no prevention/community-health review, you are under-sampling a major blueprint category. 6.
