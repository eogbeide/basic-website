    For Step 1 exams on or after May 14, 2026: 14 x 30-minute blocks, no more than 20 items per block, one 8-hour day, at least 55 minutes of break time, and a 5-minute optional tutorial. Once you advance a COMLEX section, the prior section is no longer accessible.
