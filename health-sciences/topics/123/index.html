    -> SAFE SETTING E. PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGY - KNOW THE SAFETY SIGNATURE

    ClassCore use / principleHigh-yield adverse effect / monitoring logic
    SSRIs / SNRIsFirst-line across many depressive/anxiety disorders; delayed therapeutic response.GI/sexual effects, activation, discontinuation; serotonin toxicity and drug interactions. SNRIs can raise BP.
    Bupropion / mirtazapine / trazodoneAlternative antidepressant profiles chosen by comorbidity and adverse-effect tradeoffs.Bupropion lowers seizure threshold and is avoided in relevant eating/seizure risk; mirtazapine can increase appetite/sedation; trazodone sedation/priapism.
    TCAs / MAOIsEffective but generally later-line because of toxicity/interactions.TCA anticholinergic/cardiac toxicity in overdose; MAOI tyramine/serotonergic interactions and washout requirements.
    AntipsychoticsPsychosis, mania and selected augmentation; typical vs atypical profiles.EPS/tardive dyskinesia, NMS, hyperprolactinemia, metabolic effects, QT issues; clozapine requires blood-count monitoring because of severe neutropenia risk.
    LithiumMood stabilization and suicide-risk reduction in bipolar disorder.Narrow therapeutic index; renal/thyroid effects, tremor, DI and toxicity risk. Levels and renal/thyroid context matter; dehydration/drug interactions can increase toxicity.
    Valproate / carbamazepine / lamotrigineMood stabilizers chosen by phase/comorbidity.Valproate hepatic/platelet/teratogenic risk; carbamazepine cytopenia/hyponatremia/drug interactions; lamotrigine rash/SJS risk with rapid titration.
    Benzodiazepines / buspironeShort-term acute anxiety/agitation/withdrawal contexts vs chronic anxiolytic strategy.Benzodiazepine sedation/respiratory risk, dependence and dangerous withdrawal; buspirone has delayed onset and no rapid rescue role.
    Stimulants / ADHD medicationsAttention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder after diagnosis across settings and safety review.BP/HR, appetite/sleep and misuse/diversion considerations; screen for relevant cardiovascular/substance/mania risk.

    F. PSYCHIATRIC EMERGENCY FIRST STEPS

    PresentationFirst reasoning moveDo not miss
    Suicidal / violent riskImmediate safety, direct risk questions, collateral/lethal-means context, supervision and disposition.Intoxication, psychosis, severe mood episode, recent attempt, agitation, inability to collaborate with safety plan.
    Acute agitation / psychosis / maniaProtect patient/staff, de-escalate, evaluate medical/substance causes, then supervised medication/restraint decisions if necessary.Delirium, intoxication/withdrawal, hypoglycemia/hypoxia, neurologic disease; use least restrictive safe intervention per policy.
    Serotonin syndrome vs NMSMedication timeline + neuromuscular exam + autonomic findings; stop offending agents and escalate medical care.Serotonin: clonus/hyperreflexia; NMS: severe rigidity with dopamine-blockade context - both can be life-threatening.
    Withdrawal / overdoseABCs and toxidrome; identify alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal and opioid overdose promptly.Seizures/delirium tremens, respiratory depression, co-ingestants, electrolyte/glucose problems.
    Catatonia / deliriumRecognize syndrome and urgent medical evaluation; avoid assuming "behavioral" etiology.Malignant catatonia, NMS overlap, infection/metabolic/medication causes and inability to maintain intake.
