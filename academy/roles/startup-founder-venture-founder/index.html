    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Build a venture from zero to an evidence-backed launch. Define a founder thesis and target customer; conduct 15-20 customer interviews; quantify the market and strongest pain; ship a narrow MVP; instrument activation, retention and willingness-to-pay; close the first 3-5 customers or equivalent demand proof; build pricing and unit economics; create a 12-month cash/runway model and cap-table scenarios; prepare a fundraising data room and investor narrative; define hiring/culture principles and weekly operating metrics; add one AI-native workflow or product capability with evaluation and human-control boundaries; then make a scale, pivot or stop decision from evidence.

    Proof Rubric

    Show founder evidence, not pitch-deck theater: traceable customer learning; rapid but disciplined MVP iteration; retention / revenue or credible demand signal; explicit PMF hypothesis and disconfirming evidence; repeatable founder-led sales; transparent unit economics and runway; ownership / financing literacy; high-bar hiring and operating cadence; AI leverage with evals, security and human review; and the ability to change direction when evidence weakens the original thesis.
