Credential Taxonomy — Call It What the Provider Calls It

Verified Micro-Credential

Provider-issued

University Course Certificate

Not a degree

Completion Certificate / Badge

Do not call it certification

Provider record of successful course completion or achievement.

Audit Note

Google Skills – Professional Agentic Architect Certification path: this is an excellent preparation path, but the professional certification exam itself is not free. Google currently lists the beta exam at $120, a 40% discount from the $200 retail price. The Google Skills Starter tier is no-cost and currently includes 35 free credits each month, limited hands-on labs and skill badges. Zuyini therefore lists the free badge/preparation layer separately and does not count the Professional Agentic Architect certification as a $0 certification.

Resume / LinkedIn naming rule: List the credential exactly as the issuing provider names it. Example: “Microsoft Applied Skills: Generate reports with AI research agents”, not “Microsoft Certified AI Engineer.” Likewise, an OpenLearn Statement of Participation, Kaggle completion certificate, AWS badge or Salesforce Superbadge should not be renamed as a professional certification.

Zuyini Audit Standard for This Directory

  1. Prefer official provider pages.
  2. Verify that the credential itself — not only the training — is free.
  3. Separate certification, micro-credential, course certificate and badge.
  4. Flag subscriptions, lab credits, product-tier prerequisites and exam fees.
  5. Re-check links and pricing before each Academy release.
  6. Preserve the provider's exact credential name.
  7. Pair credentials with hands-on proof so the Academy remains skills-first, not badge-first.
