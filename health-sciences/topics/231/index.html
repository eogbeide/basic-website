    WHY STUDENTS MISS IT Learners often default to their favorite resource regardless of the actual failure. EASIEST CLUE Name the error before opening the resource. — F. 48-72 HOUR RETEST + RECURRENCE RULE

    • After repair, use a new prompt rather than the exact item. Exact-item recognition can overstate learning.
    • Retest after a delay, then again inside a mixed block. A repair that works only in a focused set is incomplete.
    • If the concept fails again, ask whether the missing piece is upstream (e.g., physiology before pathology, anatomy before localization, receptor signaling before pharmacology).
    • Promote a topic out of the active weak list only after it survives new-item transfer; continue light spaced maintenance.
