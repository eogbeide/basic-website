    LEARNING ROUTEWHAT IT DOES
    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONSBuild anatomy, cellular/molecular science, biochemistry, physiology, microbiology, immunology, pharmacology, pathology, clinical skills, evidence-based medicine, ethics and OPP/OMM (DO).
    YEAR 2 - SYSTEMS + DISEASEIntegrate organ-system physiology, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, diagnosis and therapeutics; prepare for Step 1 / COMLEX Level 1.
    YEAR 3 - CORE CLERKSHIPSLearn supervised patient care, clinical reasoning and specialty knowledge while preparing for NBME Subject Exams / COMAT and Step 2 / Level 2.
    YEAR 4 - ADVANCED CLINICALSub-internship, emergency/critical care, electives, advanced patient management, residency readiness and transition to internship.
    LICENSING EXAMSUSMLE Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3; COMLEX-USA Level 1, Level 2-CE and Level 3; NBME clinical subject exams and COMAT.
