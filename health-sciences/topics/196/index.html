    K. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION INTEGRATED CASES

    #CASEANSWER LOGIC
    1Loop shows higher ESV after acute pressure load.Afterload increased: ventricle ejects less -> ESV rises; do not call this reduced preload.
    2Systolic murmur gets louder with lower LV volume.Think dynamic obstruction/MVP physiology; use chamber/loading logic before memorized maneuvers.
    3Dyspnea + low O2, high A-a gradient, poor response to O2.True shunt becomes more likely than simple hypoventilation or low V/Q.
    4FEV1/FVC low with hyperinflation.Obstructive physiology: flow problem first; then distinguish asthma/COPD from reversibility/history.
    5Normal ratio but TLC and DLCO low.Restrictive parenchymal process is favored over pure chest-wall/neuromuscular restriction.
    6ACE inhibitor started in bilateral renal-artery stenosis; creatinine rises.Efferent dilation lowers glomerular pressure/GFR in a kidney dependent on angiotensin-II-mediated efferent tone.
    7Loop diuretic causes hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis.Segment effect -> increased distal Na delivery + volume contraction/aldosterone -> K/H+ loss.
    8AKI with muddy brown casts after shock.Localize to tubular injury after ischemia; sediment beats vague BUN/Cr pattern memorization.
    9COPD with chronic hypercapnia has elevated HCO3-.Kidneys retain bicarbonate over time: appropriate chronic respiratory-acidosis compensation.
    10Decompensated HF has edema yet RAAS is activated.Effective arterial volume is low despite high total-body fluid; renal response follows perceived perfusion, not edema appearance.
