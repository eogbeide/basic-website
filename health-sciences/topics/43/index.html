    Content review recipe: 60-120 minutes per weak topic

    • Retrieve first: write/draw the pathway, anatomy, differential or management algorithm from memory before opening notes.
    • Repair the mechanism: use a substantive video or core text only for what you cannot explain.
    • Compress: create a one-page mechanism map, comparison table or illness script.
    • Apply: complete 10-25 targeted questions immediately; review every distractor.
    • Retest later: repeat missed concepts after 48-72 hours and again in a mixed block the following week.
