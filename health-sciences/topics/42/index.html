    REVIEW EXPANSION - V2

    This expansion adds a second learning layer: structured content review for school exams plus dedicated USMLE, COMLEX, Shelf/NBME and COMAT review tracks. The goal is not resource accumulation; it is repeated retrieval, application, assessment and targeted remediation. What changes in v2

    • Every major phase now has a review layer: block review -> cumulative review -> professional-exam review.
    • Review is blueprint-driven: official USMLE/NBOME/NBME specifications determine what must remain retrievable.
    • Video review is role-specific: deep concept videos for weak mechanisms, concise board review for consolidation, clinical videos for reasoning and bedside skills.
    • Question banks remain central: content review is followed immediately by vignette practice; explanations become the remediation map.
    • Readiness uses multiple signals: school performance, question-bank trends, official self-assessments, timing/endurance and error stability.
