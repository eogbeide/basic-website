Integrative Capstone

Investigate a metabolic or molecular mechanism using a reproducible evidence package. Build a pathway and structure-function map; state a mechanistic hypothesis; select biochemical assays and controls; analyze kinetic or metabolic data; trace energy/redox balance; predict effects of a mutation, inhibitor or nutrient perturbation; compare alternative explanations; and produce a research-style report with figures, uncertainty and follow-up experiments.

Evidence Standard

  • Molecular accuracy
  • quantitative enzyme reasoning
  • pathway integration
  • bioenergetic bookkeeping
  • mechanistic causality
  • appropriate controls
  • data interpretation
  • uncertainty
  • reproducibility
  • ability to connect molecular perturbation to cellular phenotype. RELEASE QUALITY STANDARD: long-form academic instruction, explicit limitations and an inspectable capstone artifact - not just claimed
