    BIOSTATISTICS, EPIDEMIOLOGY & EVIDENCE-BASED MEDICINE

    YEAR 1 - FOUNDATIONS | Curriculum + Exam Mastery

    WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

    Become fluent enough in quantitative reasoning to interpret studies, screening tests and clinical evidence under time pressure.

    Mastery checklist

    • Study designs, hierarchy of evidence and causal inference basics.
    • Bias, confounding, effect modification and validity.
    • Risk, odds, incidence, prevalence, relative/absolute risk, NNT/NNH.
    • Sensitivity, specificity, predictive values and likelihood concepts.
    • Confidence intervals, p-values, power and common statistical tests conceptually.
    • Diagnostic studies, screening, systematic reviews and guideline interpretation.

    You should be able to...

    • Identify study design and major threats to validity.
    • Calculate and interpret core epidemiologic measures.
    • Interpret screening/diagnostic test results in context of prevalence.
    • Read abstracts, tables and forest plots efficiently.
    • Translate evidence into a patient-centered decision.
