    Role-Readiness Proof / Capstone

    Design and validate a production-ready compute or embedded board: requirements/block diagram, component/BOM choices, schematic and PCB constraints, I2C/SPI/USB or high-speed interfaces, SI/PI and power/thermal budgets, bring-up checklist, automated validation, DFM/DFT plus EVT/DVT/PVT plan, reliability qualification, root-cause report and NPI release criteria.

    Proof Rubric

    Show traceability from requirements to architecture, schematic and layout; justify components and SI/PI/power/thermal tradeoffs; bring up hardware methodically with measurements; automate repeatable validation; demonstrate DFM/DFT and reliability evidence; isolate failures to root cause; define production-ready release criteria.
