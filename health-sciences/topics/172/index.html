    THE TEST IS NOT "DO I KNOW THE THREE LAYERS?" THE TEST IS WHETHER YOU CAN CHOOSE THE RIGHT LAYER AND INTERVENTION WHEN PERFORMANCE DATA CONFLICT. I. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION CASES

    #ScenarioBest review move
    1A locally emphasized anatomy structure is on Friday's practical but rarely appears on your board Qbank.Layer 1 now: master the school requirement; keep only proportional later maintenance.
    2You can explain renal physiology immediately after a video but cannot reconstruct it the next morning.Retrieval/retention problem: closed-book reconstruction + spaced re-test, not another full video.
    3Current endocrine block is strong, but cardiology from two months ago disappears in mixed questions.Layer 2 failed: restore a weekly prior-block spiral; do not restart endocrine.
    4You know the diagnosis but keep choosing confirmatory tests when the vignette already provides enough evidence to treat.Reasoning/sequence repair: build task ladder and practice next-step questions.
    5You finish the Qbank but many correct answers were low-confidence guesses.Not done: review unstable corrects; completion volume is not mastery evidence.
    6A school test rewards detailed lab methodology that is not central to the national exam.Learn it for Layer 1; compress or drop later unless it supports broader mechanism.
    7After first learning, you score well in blocked acid-base sets but poorly when acid-base is mixed with respiratory and renal cases.Interleave: the remaining gap is strategy selection/discrimination.
    8You miss a pathology image despite knowing the disease facts.Representation gap: image/practical exposure + verbal feature discrimination, not broad disease rereading.
    9Official assessment improves, but the error log still shows repeated management-sequence mistakes.Layer 3 is incomplete: repair repeated sequence errors and re-test on new items.
    10You want to add a second full review course because progress feels slow, although recent data show only two recurring weak domains.Do not expand the stack: target the two weak domains with one defined repair resource each.

    J. THREE-LAYER DECISION RULE

    QuestionIf YESIf NO
    Is the task required by the current school block/rotation?Layer 1 gets immediate priority.Move to retention/board relevance.
    Was the concept once learned but is now inaccessible?Layer 2: spaced retrieval + new-item re-test.Diagnose knowledge vs reasoning vs representation.
    Is the goal a national exam or cumulative standardized assessment?Layer 3: blueprint + mixed timed practice + official calibration.Keep the intervention local and specific.
    Did the same error recur after repair?Escalate targeted repair and re-test.Return to normal maintenance cadence.

    J. THREE-LAYER DECISION RULE — K. MASTERY GATE

    • Can identify the active layer before selecting a resource or study method.
    • Can retrieve first, diagnose the failure, choose a targeted repair, and re-test on new material after delay.
    • Can keep current school objectives, cumulative retention and national-exam transfer active without giving all three equal time every day.
    • Can distinguish knowledge, reasoning, sequence, representation and timing problems and choose different interventions for each.
    • Can show shrinking repeat errors, preserved older knowledge, stable timed performance and readiness evidence appropriate to the next assessment.
