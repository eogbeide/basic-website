    B. THREE LEVELS OF THE DAILY STUDY LOOP

    Day typeCore executionSuccess condition
    Full study dayClosed-book launch -> primary concept / timed mixed block -> deep review -> 1-3 targeted repairs -> delayed retest scheduling -> brief maintenance.Several high-quality cycles; stop when review quality or accuracy deteriorates rather than adding passive hours.
    School / clinical-heavy dayShort retrieval -> small question/image set -> one highest-value error repair -> one prior-topic revisit.Protect continuity and error control; do not attempt to recreate a dedicated-study day after a long mandatory day.
    Minimum viable study day10-20 min closed-book retrieval -> small fresh question/image set -> one meaningful repair -> one spaced revisit.A small complete loop is better than a large passive session or zero-contact day when capacity is compressed.

    C. DEFAULT ORDER OF OPERATIONS

    StepActionOutput
    1. Retrieve before rescueTry to reconstruct yesterday / the current mechanism before reopening notes.Shows what is actually available from memory.
    2. Do the primary taskNew learning, timed questions, practical/image work or patient-linked reasoning based on the phase.Creates the main performance signal for the day.
    3. Review the decisionExplain right answer, strongest distractor, timing/representation issue and failure mode.Turns activity into a reusable rule.
    4. Repair one layer at a timeUse the smallest resource that matches the error: mechanism, memory, sequence, image, timing or skill.Prevents broad resource expansion after a narrow miss.
    5. Schedule delayed proofAssign a fresh question, image, case or closed-book prompt after delay.Repair is not called stable until it survives without the original cue.
    6. Shut down deliberatelyCapture tomorrow's first target, close the queue and preserve recovery.Prevents endless low-output studying from displacing next-day accuracy.
