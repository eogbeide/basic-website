    E. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION POSTGRADUATE DECISIONS

    #ScenarioBest next move
    1MCQs strong; CCS weakShift marginal study time to official CCS mechanics, sequencing, monitoring and disposition.
    2CCS diagnosis right; management latePractice stabilization checkpoints and advance simulated time only after urgent actions/monitoring are in place.
    3Biostats / abstracts repeatedly weakUse focused evidence-interpretation repair, then verify in fresh mixed blocks.
    4Level 3 OPP/OMM weakAdd longitudinal osteopathic diagnosis + safety + treatment-selection retrieval; do not wait for the final week.
    5Level 3 date before 2027 changeTrain the current two-day 420-MCQ + ~26-CDM structure and two-day endurance.
    6Level 3 date on/after Jan 13, 2027Train the one-day 240-MCQ + 16-CDM structure and pooled-break strategy.
    7Qbank strong; CCMSA/COMSAE weakTreat independent calibration as a warning; inspect repeats, content selection, task mix and case performance.
    8Repeated unsafe discharge / follow-upBuild a disposition checklist: stability, pending risk, support, medication plan, follow-up and return precautions.
    9Limited study time on serviceProtect one primary bank + official cases + error log; cut duplicate videos/notes first.
    10Final week stable but anxiousConsolidate recurring errors, official interface, sleep and logistics; do not add a new broad resource.
