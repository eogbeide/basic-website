    D. INTERPRETATION: WHAT THE NUMBER DOES - AND DOES NOT - TELL YOU

    QuestionBest evidence to reportCommon overclaim
    How large is the effect/association?Effect estimate + units; absolute and relative measures when useful."Significant" used instead of magnitude.
    How precise is it?Confidence/credible interval and sample information.Point estimate treated as exact.
    Could bias/confounding explain it?Design limits, measured/unmeasured confounders, selection/information bias.Adjusted model presented as proof of causality.
    Were many analyses tried?Pre-specified vs exploratory labels; multiplicity strategy when relevant.One favorable analysis reported without the search space.
    Does it matter clinically?Baseline risk, absolute change, harms, feasibility, patient relevance.Small p-value equated with important care difference.
    Will it generalize?Population, setting, exclusions, context and transportability limits.Single-site result assumed universal.
    Is it robust?Sensitivity/alternative specifications and assumption checks.One model treated as the only possible truth.
