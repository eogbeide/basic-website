    LANEMUST RETRIEVE WITHOUT NOTESHIGH-DISCRIMINATION TRANSFER
    Malabsorption -> endocrine/boneFat or mucosal malabsorption -> vitamin/mineral deficiency -> calcium/bone/endocrine consequences.Connect GI failure to the downstream endocrine or skeletal phenotype rather than treating them as separate diseases.
    Liver -> hormones / drugsHepatic synthesis/metabolism affects proteins, hormones, glucose, lipids, coagulation and drug handling.Distinguish primary endocrine disease from altered hormone binding/metabolism in liver disease.

    K. 10 HIGH-DISCRIMINATION INTEGRATED CASES

    #CASEANSWER LOGIC
    1Chronic greasy stools + weight loss + low fat-soluble vitamins.Localize malabsorption to fat digestion/absorption; then separate bile, pancreatic and mucosal causes with the rest of the pattern.
    2AST/ALT markedly elevated; alkaline phosphatase only mildly elevated.Hepatocellular injury pattern. Do not call this “liver failure” without synthetic-function evidence such as PT/INR/albumin context.
    3Low free T4 with high TSH.Primary thyroid failure: target hormone low, pituitary trophic signal appropriately high.
    4High cortisol fails to suppress when the control system should turn off.Think autonomous/excess-axis physiology; interpret the suppression test by asking what normal feedback should do.
    5Hyperglycemia + ketones + high-anion-gap acidosis; serum K initially high.DKA: insulin deficiency and acidosis shift K out of cells despite total-body K depletion from osmotic diuresis.
    6Hypercalcemia with suppressed PTH.PTH is appropriately low; search for a non-PTH driver rather than primary hyperparathyroidism.
    7Mid-cycle estrogen rise followed by abrupt LH surge.Sustained high estrogen flips to positive feedback, triggering ovulation; this is timeline logic, not a hormone-name association.
    8Positive pregnancy test, pelvic pain, no intrauterine pregnancy on ultrasound, unstable vitals.Stability and pregnancy location dominate the sequence; ectopic rupture is a time-critical concern.
    9Third-trimester painless bleeding.Use gestational age + pain pattern + placental anatomy; placenta previa becomes a key discriminator from painful abruption.
    10Young man with testicular mass and elevated tumor marker.Use age + testicular compartment + marker pattern to discriminate germ-cell tumor categories rather than memorizing markers in isolation.
